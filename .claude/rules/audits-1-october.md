# Audit log: 28 to 31 October 2026

What each audit of the published stories and the code found and fixed, across every desk, and what it looked at and left. Read the "left" lists before investigating the same thing again. Continued in audits-2 and audits-3.

- Writing and pipeline faults, 2026-10-28 (the owner: "check for writing, styling, editing etc bugs", then "fix the bugs and look for additional bugs", then, mid-way: "it is not important to fix published stories, since i will delete all of them when my website goes public, the important thing is to fix from the source like the code, the logic etc"). So a fault found in a published story is fixed where the pipeline made it, not in the story. Close reads of the 108 stories of 18-28 October, the house checks over all 408 pieces and the newsroom's run logs found, and code now holds: (1) **a mend thrown away for the desk's trade**: from 25 to 28 October five second-pass mends that had put right every finding (Apollo's ~10% put in Mubadala Capital's announcement instead of insiders' mouths, Almarai's possible revenue stated as certain) were discarded for one long sentence or a 57-word lede, and the drafts carrying the findings went out; a refused mend now gets the revision's own rescue, `lastCut()` and `lastDeskPass()`, first (run.mjs, news and hub pieces). (2) **The house spelling table** (`fixNames()`, copydesk.mjs) now writes the decimal point («2,22 مليون برميل», copied from an Arabic source, read as 222), one spelling per outlet as its sources.json entry prints it (AGBI ran three ways, «إيه جي بي آي», «أرابيان غلف بيزنس إنسايت», a bare «AGBI»; «ذا ناشونال»، «فاينانشال تايمز»، «ديلي نيوز إيجبت»، «سبايس إكس»، «جيه بي مورغان»، «أسوشييتد برس»), a name glossed with itself; the box goes through it too (`normalizeDraft()`), and so do tags (`houseSpelling()` in tags.mjs reads the same table: «سبايس إكس» had split SpaceX's subject page); `tidy.mjs` applies it to published text in code (45 stories). A box value keeps its figure, not a list after a colon (`figureOnly()`, keyfacts.mjs: «3 أنظمة: التجاري و…»). (3) **Both sentence cutters** keep a reference whole (`refersBack()`, util.mjs: a demonstrative or «كذلك» in a sentence's first four words): `trimUnsourced()` keeps the sentence the next one points back at, the last cut takes the pointing sentence with a cut figure. (4) **Market data**: Yahoo keeps one daily bar for TASI and the EGX30, so their previous close cannot be checked, and on 25 October it was a session stale (the EGX story printed −6.85% for −3.56%): with fewer than five daily closes the market line gives the price alone (`quoteLine()`, context.mjs). Yahoo's continuous Brent series rolls to the next delivery month days before the wires stop quoting the expiring one (28 October: BZ=F was January at 111.87, December at 115.55; on the 26th a story led on the January price beside its sources' December peak and weekly gain): the line names each future's delivery month and, while the nearer contract trades, gives it too (Brent, WTI, Henry Hub; `deliveryMonths()`), and the writer reports the month its reports quote. (5) **Checks**: speech in «» after a verb of saying, five words or more, that no Arabic source carries is sent to the desk as a translation (`SPEECH_QUOTE`, verify.mjs, with the fact-check's `quoteFound()`; calibrated on 23 live stories: no real hit, two flags that were pages unreadable today, so it guards a firm rule at no cost); eight words repeated word for word between dek, lede and body is a warning (18 of 129 stories since 18 October, each a real repeat; six words fired on 49); hard calques: «الاقتصادي الأول في» for "senior economist" (after a name's comma only; 7 stories), «مرفق تحويل الملكية» for TTF; «أحادية منخفضة» a warning; «رفع الدعم … من 500 إلى 600» now caught. The critic's news rubric names the box rule: 16 of the 108 stories carried a figure of another event under «الأرقام» (a past Brent close, another company's round, a PMI's 50-point threshold). (6) **The morning round had failed three days running** (26-28 October, "cancelled"): Openverse timed out on 89 of 97 searches at 45 seconds each, seven stories' pictures took 38 minutes, and the job's 45-minute limit killed it inside its commit, so the pictures found, the photo checks and the health state were never saved and the editor never ran. Openverse now stands down for ten minutes after three failures in a row (photolibs.mjs; the newsroom's own runs had waited the same way, 27 timeouts in one), the picture step is capped at 25 minutes and never ends the round (`continue-on-error`), and the job has 75. (7) **The calendar**: bls.gov answers 403 to every request (robots.txt included) and the US CPI and jobs dates were stored ones ending 10 December; they are read from FRED's release calendar when BLS refuses (dates matched exactly; the reference month is the one before the release, none named in a month holding two releases), and the calendar names itself as the newsroom's reader, never a browser (the Fed, ECB and BoE answer it). Not code faults, left: Al-Riyadh's feed and Financial Express's news sitemap answer from home but not from GitHub's runners (cloud addresses walled); errors a source prints (Masrawy's Arab net flows as buying, AFP's Kpler figure without «يومياً», AGBI's «متحدث الحكومة اليمنية» for the coalition's) pass every check that reads against the sources; the published stories were not desked again (the owner's ruling above), though 19 corrections and the spelling tidy made before it stand. The layout of 15 page types at 390 and 1366 px was probed in headless Edge (layout-probe in that session's scratchpad): nothing scrolls sideways, is clipped, sits under 11 px, breaks or throws; the one report, the phone menu's list off the left edge, is the closed «details» menu's content, which Chrome keeps in layout but does not draw (checked on a screenshot).
- The audit of 29 October (the owner: "check for other issues in recent articles and fix them at the source if they exist"). Three independent close reads of the 21 pieces published since the audit of the 28th, every finding checked here in the text, the evidence or the code before anything changed; one claim did not hold (that a mend skips the copy desk: both mends pass it). Fixed at the source: (1) **Asharq Al-Awsat's pages read as another story.** Its pages carry three more articles below their own (infinite scroll); the own body is `entry-content first-article-node`, the others plain `entry-content`, and Readability took the longest. Its Fed, ECB and GDP pages all read as one IMF story on Iraq and Egypt that afternoon; the GDP story printed the IMF's comment on Warsh as that day's Asharq Al-Awsat report, and of the 53 of its pages kept as evidence since September, 12 share almost no word with their own headline (the PCE story's evidence is about Tullow, the Saudi market story's about a bank's sterling profit). `ownArticleOnly()` (extract.mjs) removes every block that repeats the marked article's containers without holding it, before the text, the photographs (a handout could have come from an appended story) and the fallback read the page; Asharq Business and Al Eqtisadiah, the same publisher's, were checked and read their own text; self-tested. (2) **«لماذا يهمّ» had a template**: from 26 to 29 October 19 of 42 boxes said something «يبقى رهناً/مرهوناً بـ…» or «يتحدد…» (45 of 324 before), many closing «، لا بـ…»: `WHY_POLICY` asks for the consequence in the event's own terms and for proportion (FAB's box made eight basis points on a loan book «جزء كبير» of a profit they barely touch), and `BOX_TAKEBACK` also warns on «، لا بـ…» (21 of 422 published boxes, nearly all «not X alone»). (3) **Padding passed as depth**: half the ACWA–EIG story was the partners' self-descriptions, half the Iraq–Syria pipeline story diplomacy and a transfer of detainees, a third of the UAE rate story the Fed's own story retold, partly at the critic's «ينقص التقرير» asking for them. `NEWS_DEPTH` names them padding, the critic may not ask for them or for a fact the draft already states in other words, and the desk notes tag a company's self-description, an executive's praise and a communiqué's topic list as remarks (two kept, not counted toward `NEWS_MIN_FACTS`) and leave out diplomacy and security with no bearing on the economic consequence, so a thin event goes short. (4) **The UAE story was given US markets**: `macroMarketOf()` (context.mjs) took the first table row that matched anywhere, so «…بعد تثبيت الفيدرالي» fetched the S&P 500, the ten-year yield and the dollar index; now the place the headline names first decides, and a place whose markets the feed does not quote (`NO_MARKET`: the UAE, Qatar, Kuwait…) gets none. (5) **Company results**: ADNOC Drilling called EBITDA «الأرباح التشغيلية» throughout, Samsung's lede made spending 60% above last year a plan «raised by 60%», ADNOC's new guidance at the bottom of its old ranges was «ضيّقت», Shell set oil on the year beside gas on the quarter: COMPANY RESULTS in `EDITORIAL_CRAFT`. (6) **A forecast lost its condition** after the critic had seen it restored (the World Bank's 79 dollars «في ظل افتراض… عودة تدفقات النفط» and 105 «إذا تعطلت إمدادات إضافية بنحو 5 ملايين برميل يومياً»): a rule for every writer, and the copy desk's hedge guard (`HEDGES`, copydesk.mjs) now has a third kind, the condition («إذا»، «في حال»، «افتراض»، «ما لم»، «ما دام»…), which may be reworded, never dropped; checked against 120 published stories rewritten unchanged, no refusal. (7) Smaller: a source's earlier direction word beside a later quote that moved the other way (the ECB story: "the dollar extended gains" beside the euro up 0.06%); the reviser takes an editor's suggested wording as a direction, not copy (an analysis box pasted from its critic contradicted its headline), and checks a "missing" fact is not already there (Alphabet's capex told twice); the box's labels name their period and a forecast as one; «سلباً على التضخم» warned (it read as worse inflation where the ECB meant dampened); «الشرق للأعمال» and «اقتصاد الشرق مع بلومبرغ» are «الشرق بلومبرغ» and «غوغل» «جوجل» in the spelling table; «المصرف المركزي الإماراتي» leads to «مصرف الإمارات المركزي»'s tag page (tag-aliases.json). Looked at and left: widening the headline country check to explainers and analyses (no hit in 56 of them), Brent for every Hormuz story (it would pad the diplomatic ones), a check of framing against sources in the fact-check (a change there restarts the lessons' evidence pools), the 41-45-word sentences the long-sentence check lets through, and errors a source itself prints (Microsoft's adjusted EPS below its reported one while "excluding a loss", in CNBC too). (8) **Egypt's first rate rise since March 2024 was never written.** Asharq Al-Awsat's appended story reported the Central Bank of Egypt raising its rates by 100 basis points on Thursday 29 October; the evening editor chose it («المركزي المصري يرفع الفائدة 1% في أول زيادة منذ مارس 2024») and the newsroom skipped it as a repeat of «المركزي الباكستاني يرفع الفائدة إلى 12% في أول زيادة منذ أبريل»: `sameEvent()` (lib/events.mjs) saw «المركزي» lead both and enough shared words. Headlines that each name a country, and none in common, are now two events (`countriesNamed()`, lib/regions.mjs, reads NATIONALITIES' places and nisbas from a word's start, so «مشتركة» is not Turkish). Over every pair of the 372 published news stories within four days, the four true repeats still match and no pair changed. The items took the ordinary twelve-hour mark, so a run after about 06:40 UTC on the 30th may take the event again. Tested on two stories rewritten from their re-fetched sources (harnesses in that session's scratchpad): the Iraq–Syria pipeline story lost its detainees and, after a second wording of the notes rule, its diplomacy (first visit, New York meeting, airport) and border; but its notes still split about eight facts into 15 (the line's age and its outage, the communiqué's trade and farming topics), so it stays above `NEWS_MIN_FACTS`, is written long, and the critic, now told what padding is, rejected the 311 words as unable to carry 300 economic ones. A thin event can therefore still be lost rather than run short: the open question is whether a second-pass verdict of padding should send the story to the short form instead of rejecting it.

- Bugs, a second round the same night (the owner: "check for more bugs and fix them"). Two independent reviews of the code of 28 and 29 October, every finding reproduced with a probe before it was fixed and pinned by a self-test that fails on the old code. (1) **The sentence cutters**: `refersBack()` (util.mjs) read «هذا العام»/«في هذه الأثناء» as pointing back, so the last cut took a sourced forecast with the unsourced figure before it (a demonstrative before a word of time names the time); it now also reads «وهو ما…»، «مما…»; both cutters split at every full stop, even inside «…», so a cut could leave half a quotation (`sentenceUnits()` keeps a quotation over several sentences as one unit); the trim's references back stopped at a field's edge (the lede and body are read on).
