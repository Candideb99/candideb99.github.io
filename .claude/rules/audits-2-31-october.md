# Audit log: the second and third audits of 31 October 2026

Continues audits-1-october.md.

- The second audit of 31 October (the owner: "check other articles on the websites if they show symptoms of new bugs and fix the bugs at the source. also check the control room"). No piece was newer than the first audit's, so four independent readers took the older ones on one axis each (facts against the saved evidence for 23-28 October, the photographs of 20-28 October looked at and traced through the logs, the built pages of 20-28 October and the page types not read before, and the control room's code, probed with GET requests on its own instance), and a dry newsroom round ran on the new code; every finding verified here, each fix pinned by a self-test that fails on the old code. Fixed: (1) **the plan's session limit**. The readers and the dry run spent the subscription's five-hour allowance at 03:24 UTC (no cloud run fell before its reset at 04:30; the next started at 05:52 and published cleanly), and the dry run showed what a refused run does: the Claude CLI's «You've hit your session limit» was a blip to retry, three tries a step, and two chosen stories failed and took the rejection mark, losing one of their two chances to a refusal that was never about them. `CLAUDE_LIMIT` (llm.mjs) makes it `meta.quota`, the flag the newsroom already threaded through the precheck, the picture desk, the desk notes and the mends for the ChatGPT allowance: no retry, the story and the rest of the run wait unmarked (run.mjs, «deferred: the model's allowance is spent»), the second look stops without counting a failure against the story it was reading (two and it would be read no more), and a story it had flagged is read again next run, since "flagged" is never reopened and its correction would meet the same refusal (recheck.mjs). An audit of this size is itself a heavy user of the allowance the newsroom shares. (2) **The site's figures took the sentence's full stop**: «\d[\d.,]*» isolated «17.» as a figure and the line drew «إلى .17 وتقول» on 196 pages (342 places). A figure now ends on a digit; a percent may stand before a range's dash, spaced or not («3.75%-4%» had read «4%-3.75%» on 13 pages); a Latin run gives back a sentence's full stop and a bracket it did not open or close («(تقدير Kpler)»), and digits joined by a hyphen to a word stay in its run («10-year») (`latinParts()`, bidi.ts and rehype-isolate-numbers.mjs; the self-test also holds the two copies of the patterns equal). (3) **The saved evidence lost quotes**: in `passagesOf()` (snapshots.mjs) a dangling `else` dropped every quote the page's sentences did not hold as written, the figure sentences filled the 4,000-character cap in page order before the quotes, the first piece too long for the room left ended the copy, and glued pages stayed one "sentence" (143 of 1,775 quotes found at publication were missing; a BBC source was saved empty). Quoted sentences now go first, then the figures', past any oversized piece; glued sentences are split without cutting an initial («Lisa D. Cook»); a quote inside a run the splitter cannot open keeps itself. The second look's protections read these passages. (4) **A disagreement between sources was corrected as an error**: the Saudi tariff support, «مع بدء تطبيق التعريفة الجديدة» as Asharq Al-Awsat had it at publication, was "corrected" toward The National's "ahead of tariff changes". `settleChanged()` now calls a contradiction from another source than the one that bore the sentence out at publication a conflict, listed and never corrected (the saved copy's source list names the backer). (5) **Hub pieces never saved evidence**: the analyses, readings and weeklies are checked before publication but kept no copy, so the second look read them with nothing to tell a rewritten page from an error; `finishHubPiece()` saves it as a news story does. (6) **Pictures**: one photograph under two identities (a Commons file imported from Flickr keeps the Flickr number in brackets and is now that Flickr photo; a «(cropped)» file is its file, in `photoKey()` and `seriesKey()`; the two published pairs, the Singapore tanker and Zuckerberg's F8 keynote, are now found by `sharedPhotos()`); `CENTRAL_BANK` did not know Pakistan's State Bank (its photograph was refused under its own rate rise and the story ran without one), Bank Indonesia, Bank Negara or a Banco Central; a held-back snapshot had never been read for its subject or clarity (the verdict is one word, AMATEUR): it is read once more with the standard set aside and held only when that passes; a sovereign rating of a country and the state's own deficit («العجز المالي», «بلوغ العجز سقفه») are the state's accounts in `treasuryStory()` (5 more of 448 headlines, each one; a company's rating, a trade deficit and a bond yield are not). (7) **The control room**: it never took the cloud's commits unless he pressed Sync, so it drafted events the cloud had already published (it now syncs when opened from OPEN_CONTROL_ROOM.cmd and before every run it starts; a `--sections` choice is added to the newsroom step, not to the shared sync command); Publish left out the story's evidence and its handout photograph, which ran as a broken image (`filesOf()`); Unpublish pushed `-A content/articles`, every local draft with the one story; a discarded local draft's "do not come back" mark stayed on the laptop and its evidence and photograph behind; a published draft kept the hour it was written and was stamped as updated; "Make it the lead" did nothing on a story older than 48 hours, which the site counted from publication (`featuredAt`, written by the desk and read by `pickLead()`, so the 48 hours run from the click); the deploy light read the newest run, so every running deploy showed "THE SITE IS NOT UPDATING", and was never read again after the page loaded (it now judges the last finished deploy, says "deploying now", and refreshes every five minutes outside Settings); the run counter and the last run's box missed stories held, failed or left for the next run (the second-pass critic's rejection now prints a `reject` line); a finished job showed only "Finished" or "Stopped or failed" (the note now carries what it did or why it stopped); Settings saved during a job said "goes live with the next publish", which never happened (it now refuses before writing); the Claude model offered the retired 3.5 models of 2024 and reached only `KHAZENDAR_MODEL`, which the cloud does not read for Claude (it now sets `KHAZENDAR_CLAUDE_MODEL`); the chat's model menu offered Gemini and GPT to Claude Code and its "Default (same as newsroom)" passed no model at all; with a subscription token the chat was also given an API key, which Claude Code bills first; the provider menu called the active Claude "(Idle / Standby)"; the chat's "Publish to the live site" pushed code without the gate (it runs `scripts/gate.mjs` first); the month filter was never filled; the stopped «في العمق» had a coverage row reading "last 36 days ago" (its button stays, marked stopped); the time estimate said 8-10 minutes for four stories (about half an hour); and the Where column still put short stories on the cover and called the next five "the ticker", which left the front on 19 October (it now follows the front's modules: lead, cover, readings, second stories, «الأخبار في سطور», top stories; the section blocks lower down are not mirrored). (8) **Smaller**: the article page's strip repeated a story «اقرأ أيضاً» printed (55 pages; it passes `printed`); a subject page took any story with one of its tags anywhere in the list (the oil page listed gold, the Fed and Riyadh airport; 130 such listings): from another section only with the tag among its first two or in its headline (`topicArticles()`); commodities had no subject tags, so «الذهب» was a file beside «السلع والمعادن», and «البنوك», «أسواق الدين», «عوائد السندات», «خام دبي», «خام عُمان», «أسواق النفط» join their subjects; aviation's label «الطيران» stood over Bahri's shipping («الطيران والنقل»); growth reads «نمواً» and the PMI; «46%-» in a box is «−46%» (`figureOnly()`); a chart of revenue beside net profit is two measures (`MEASURE_FAMILIES`; SpaceX's, the one such of all published charts); /tags/ counted the Gulf at 158 where its page lists 159; «لوزارة الدفاع» linked «الدفاع» to the defence industry (a word after وزارة، وزير، هيئة، مجلس is a name, rehype-topic-links.mjs); a correction note said «من هذا الخبر» under an analysis or an explainer (correct.mjs names the kind). Looked at and left: a source credited on the page that was never read (6 stories; no story needed it to make two outlets); a chart's values are matched against the sources' figures but not read by the fact-check; two attributions blended by the checker's own judgement (the Guardian's figures credited to the FT); bare numbers in a box («2»); the Commons credit when a record names no usable author; `FRONT` dropping a tanker whose description mentions a skyline behind it; the series rule keeping Dario Amodei's only free photographs off every later Anthropic story; Daily News Egypt's and Saudi Gazette's feeds quiet since 28 and 29 October (their own sites stopped too); `.claude/launch.json` expects the control room on 4777 while it takes 7777 when free.
- The third audit of 31 October (the owner: "check for other bugs and issues and fix them and push the fixes without having to ask me"). From the day's run logs, the editor's round and two independent reviews of the code changed since 30 October, every finding checked here first. (1) **Sky News Arabia's business feed had stopped**: the business desk moved to its own site, snabusiness.com, on 29 October (skynewsarabia.com/business redirects there) and /rss/business.xml stood still at 29 October 16:59 UTC, so every run read "0 fresh items" from it for two days. `skynewsarabia` now reads the feed the new site names in its head (/rss/iqtisad/rss.xml, out of date order and reaching back to 2025, which the freshness window filters); it is still one outlet with the Middle East feed, since outlets are counted by name. (2) **A feed that stops is now seen**: `fetchFeed()` tells `onRead` its newest item of any age (never one dated more than six hours ahead), run.mjs keeps each feed's last answer in `pipeline/state/feeds.json`, and the morning round opens one issue for the owner listing the news feeds read in the last two days whose newest item is three days old (the editor cannot read the web to find where a feed went). A scan of every enabled feed that afternoon found none past 72 hours (Daily News Egypt, quiet as a site since 28 October, the nearest at 67). (3) **The reports desk**: the World Bank's October Commodity Markets Outlook was read twice as "0 chars, no PDF" and left. Its address on the Open Knowledge Repository (…/bitstreams/<uuid>/download) is a script page, 565 KB of HTML with no text; `documentUrl()` reads the file from the repository's API (…/server/api/core/bitstreams/<uuid>/content, which its robots.txt allows: 70 pages). Its summary heading came out of the PDF letter-spaced («E X E C U T I V E S U M M A RY»), so `reportExcerpts()` took the copyright page and acknowledgments as the summary; a heading is now also looked for with its spaces taken out. And the news desk had told that edition on 29 October («البنك الدولي يتوقع ارتفاع أسعار الطاقة 32% في 2026», the report named in its body only), which the reports desk, reading headlines alone, did not see: had the PDF been readable, the report would have run twice. `coveredBy()` also takes a news story whose headline or lede names the publisher and whose text names the report (never for a monthly release, whose names are common phrases); replayed over every published story it marks only the two World Bank editions the site told and the release the old rule already caught. The edition is marked covered in the reports state. (4) **The owner's issues went stale**: the morning round opens an issue once per title and never touched it again, so the corrections issue of 27 September still listed its first story (the round found three), and «the daily editor could not fix "stall"» stayed open from 6 October, weeks after stories ran again; since an open issue is never opened twice, a new stall would have raised no alarm. An open issue's text is now kept current (`gh issue edit`), and a «could not fix» issue is closed with a comment once its finding is gone (the round opens a new one if it returns). (5) **Our own desks disagreed on TTF**: style.mjs orders the gas benchmark written «عقود TTF الهولندية», but TTF was not among the Latin tokens the checks allow, so it drew a warning and the critic's «ولا يزال «TTF» غير معرّب», one of the four reasons the European gas story was rejected after revision. TTF and JKM join `ALLOWED_LATIN`, and the critic is told the benchmark codes the Arab desks print in Latin (TTF, JKM, WTI, S&P 500) and a company's own Latin name (stc) are never to be Arabized. From the two reviews, each reproduced here and pinned by a self-test that fails on the old code: (6) **`settleChanged()` could never correct a sentence supported at publication**: it compared the evidence copy's Arabic source name («الشرق بلومبرغ») with the check's English one («Asharq Business»), so every such contradiction, a real error the check before publication had missed included, read as another source's and was listed as a conflict; my own test had used Arabic on both sides. It compares the two sources by address now (recheck passes the page's list), else by either name. (7) **The evidence keeper still lost quotes**: each quote's neighbouring sentences sat in one set with the quotes, taken in page order, so early neighbours used the 4,000 characters before later quotes (the Hespress source of the Morocco trade story lost a quoted sentence carrying 750.56 and 597.64); every quoted sentence now goes first, then loose quotes, neighbours and figures. (8) **The control room's new sync broke itself**: `git pull --rebase --autostash` exits 0 when putting the desk's own changes back conflicts and leaves markers; every local run rewrites pipeline/runs/latest.json and every cloud run commits it, so the markers landed there and the next sync failed with "unresolved conflict", stopping every job (reproduced in a test repository). `scripts/sync.mjs` is the sync now: the run summary is the cloud's again before pulling (each run keeps its own report), anything still unmerged takes the cloud's version with this copy's kept in `git stash` and the job stops saying so, and a half-done rebase is undone (tested in scratch repositories: the summary case, a real conflict, a sync after it, and a rebase stopped by a file the cloud deleted). The job counter read `--limit` from the job's first line, now the pull's, and showed "1 written" for "1 of 4 written". (9) **«اقرأ أيضاً» counted two names of one subject as two topics** («النفط» and «خام برنت» tied a Brent story to Aramco's work-from-home notice; 56 of 1,273 links): `onTheSameTopic()` counts distinct subjects. (10) **Subject pages read the headline as a string**: «النفط» matched «غير النفطية», and a subject named in a cause clause put gold «مع رهانات رفع الفائدة» on the monetary page and China's exports «مدفوعة بالذكاء الاصطناعي» on the AI page; `topicArticles()` now reads the headline's main clause in whole words (`termPattern()`), and the oil page excludes «رحلات الناقلات» and «الناقلات الجوية» (an airline's carriers). Over the archive the headline-only listings went from 33 to 24 (14 dropped, 5 added: a whole word also matches its «لل» form, «للفائدة»، «للاستثمار»), each kept one about its subject. (11) **«أرقام اليوم»** dropped a different fact that shared a value («12%» Pakistan's rate and the real's fall): the same value is the same fact only when the labels share half their words. (12) **A lone «&» joined a Latin run** («Apple & غوغل» drew it on the wrong side): a spaced «&» or dash joins only between two Latin words («Johnson & Johnson», «Brent - WTI»); all 617 Latin runs of the published text came out the same. (13) **`treasuryStory()`** read household costs as parts of words anywhere in the headline, so «بدعم النمو» in Britain's budget story, «مرسوم الميزانية» and «… مع تراجع أسعار النفط» hid budgets, and «عجزاً مالياً» was no deficit; and a company's rating named with its nationality («تصنيف «أرامكو» السعودية») passed as the state's. Household costs are whole words of the main clause (never support for growth or the price of oil, gold, money or shares), the deficit takes its accusative, and a sovereign rating names a country right after «تصنيف»; over 457 headlines only Britain's budget story changed. (14) `CENTRAL_BANK` held the National Bank of Egypt (a commercial bank) and matched Dubai's Sama Tower. (15) «قام ب» and «لعب دور» from a word's start had let «سيقوم ب», «لتقوم بدور», «سيلعب دوراً» through (no new hit in the archive). (16) The desk notes counted answers, not tries: after a timeout and an unreadable answer the last try still asked for job tags and the writer got no notes; `chat()` now tells a validator its attempt. (17) A rate decision written with vowel marks («يُثبّت»، «يُخفّض») fetched no market and no central bank's building: both read the headline without its marks. Looked at and left: the copy desk's number guard refusing a rewrite for a count in letters («مرتين», «ربعين»; 18 refusals in the day's logs, each a field kept as the reviser wrote it); Openverse's breaker costing about 45 seconds each time it opens again after its ten minutes; a Commons category the planner names in other words («Filling stations in …» where Commons says «Petrol stations in …»); two of the day's eight news stories running the held amateur photograph as the last resort (his rule of 30 October); a person's title removed because no source gave it (Russia's diesel story named Alexander Novak bare: identification is sourced); GitHub's notice that the checkout, setup-node, upload-artifact and deploy-pages actions run on Node 24 in place of Node 20 (they work); a story rejected after revision for one sentence over 40 words that the last desk pass left (the World Bank outlook of 28 October, told by the next run); an abbreviation's dot («Apple Inc.») given back to the Arabic line like a sentence's full stop (none in the published text; keeping it inside would misplace a sentence's own full stop); the topic links' name lookbehind taking any letters before «هيئة», so «تهيئة» would block a link after it (none in the published text).
