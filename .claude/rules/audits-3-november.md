# Audit log: Codex's reviews and the bug scans of November 2026

Continues audits-2-31-october.md.

- Codex's review, 2026-11-01 (the owner forwarded its five findings: "codex claims to have found bugs"). All five reproduced before anything changed; each fix is pinned by a self-test that fails on the old code. (1) **A publish from the desk committed the whole index** (`git add` then a plain `git commit`), so a file staged by hand or by the chat went live with the story; every desk commit, «Publish changes» included, takes its own paths alone (`commitSteps()`, scripts/desk-git.mjs, `git commit -- paths`), and anything else stays staged; a rename or a removal staged by hand still goes out whole (`git add` refuses an already staged removal, which had also stopped «Publish changes» before). (2) **A failed photo search was remembered as searched**: the picture desk carries on past a failed planner, judge, check or caption, so its «nothing fits» could mean «not looked at», and the morning round left the story alone for good. Every desk model call goes through `ask()` (images.mjs), and `pickImage()` tells `onError` when one failed and no photo came; library timeouts are not counted (Openverse fails by nature; Commons answered all 512 searches of the last three rounds). (3) **The reports desk ignored `DAILY_CAP`** though its stories are news: it stops at the cap, and an edition held back keeps its tries. (4) **Search**: «المزيد» tapped while a new search loaded listed the old search's next twenty under the new count (22 under «نتيجتان»); a page is listed only into the list it was read for. (5) **The chat's Undo compared status codes**: a file changed before the chat and again by it was never undone, and one it did undo went back to HEAD, losing the earlier work. It now compares bytes against a snapshot taken when the message is sent, puts back what was there, and leaves the newsroom's files (articles, state, run reports) alone: a run during a chat had its state reverted by Undo. The pipeline self-test also deletes its scratch folders now (1,811 had piled up in Temp).
- Codex's second review, the same day ("codex says it found additional bugs"), five more, all reproduced and pinned the same way. (1) **`backfill-images.mjs --dry-run --apply=`** wrote the saved photographs into the stories; a dry run lists them and writes nothing. (2) **The desk's sync kept its own lines over the cloud's** (`git pull --rebase -X theirs` in scripts/sync.mjs: a fix the cloud made to a line the desk had also changed was dropped and the sync reported success). Without `-X theirs` a real clash stops the sync before anything changes and names its files; the state files still merge entry by entry (sync.mjs sets the driver's git config when a checkout lacks it), and the desk's commits leave out `pipeline/runs/latest.json` (`RUN_REPORTS` in control-room.mjs), the run summary both sides rewrite, which the sync already takes from the cloud. The cloud newsroom's own pull keeps `-X theirs`: its commits are its own run's output, never code. (3) **`repeatsInstrument()`** (context.mjs) held any markets or energy story naming an instrument, so Qatar starting a gas field would have been held behind the day's US gas price; both headlines must now report a price move (`PRICE_WORDS`). No published story or kept run report changes. (4) **`yahooQuote()`** read `regularMarketPrice: null` as a price of 0 (`Number(null)`); an empty price or time is no price. (5) **The ChatGPT quota pool** let a reading with the five-hour window alone erase a saved, still-spent weekly window and called the spent account; `withKnownLimits()` (codex-pool.mjs) keeps a window the service left out until its own reset, in routing and in the desk's Refresh.
- Codex's third review, the same day ("codex says it found more bugs"), four claims, three real, each reproduced and pinned. (1) **Drafts as material**: `loadExistingArticles()` (article.mjs) dropped the `draft` flag, so an explainer, analysis, weekly, feature or rewrite could cite a story written for review, at `/articles/<slug>/`, a page the site never builds. The loader keeps the flag; `recentNews()` and `weekStories()` (moved there from run.mjs) and the other three pickers of hub material leave drafts out; the repeat checks and the daily cap still count them. (2) **The chat with another provider**: the control room's chat ran Claude Code only when Claude wrote the news, and otherwise a text model told to change the site and report what it changed. It now runs Claude Code whenever Claude Code is installed with a Claude token or key (`chatRoute()`, scripts/desk-chat.mjs), whatever writes the news, so it spends the Claude plan; without Claude Code the newsroom's provider answers under `ANSWER_ONLY_RULES`, which say it changes nothing. (3) **Topic links**: a tag inside «…» stopped the whole paragraph's linking (`outsideQuotes()`, rehype-topic-links.mjs, goes on past it). Not changed: "market evidence can exceed the five-source limit". The cap of five (news-evidence.mjs) holds the reports and the document a story is told from; the price line comes on top of it on purpose (`eventSourcesOf()` leaves it out), or the busiest market stories, those with five reports, would lose their latest price. No published story has had more than five sources (29 carry a price line, none with five reports beside it), and no schema or page caps them; run.mjs says so where the line is added.
- Codex's sixth review, the same day ("codex reports these"), three security faults, all reproduced and pinned in scripts/pipeline-selftest.mjs. (1) **A malformed request stopped the control room**: the address was parsed before the door and outside every handler, so «GET //[» from any page open in the browser ended the process; it is answered 400. (2) **An address from content could reach this machine or a private network**: a feed's link or a page's redirect to 127.0.0.1 was read as a source, and its text could reach a story and the public evidence. `fetchPublic()` (util.mjs) resolves the host of every hop and refuses loopback, private, link-local (a cloud's metadata service), carrier, multicast and reserved addresses, following redirects itself; it serves every fetch of an address that came from content (pages and their robots files, feeds, sources' photographs and handouts, paper PDFs, report pages and documents), while the model providers (a local model among them) and the libraries' fixed APIs keep `fetchWithTimeout()`. A name that answers differently between the check and the fetch is not caught. (3) **Bodies were read whole before their size was checked** (a 7.8 KB compressed feed grew to 8 MB first): `readCapped()` stops at the cap while the bytes arrive, for feeds (8 MB), pages (6 MB, then the first 3 million characters as before), thumbnails (4 MB) and handouts (15 MB). Live after the change: a BBC page through its http→https redirect, Asharq Al-Awsat's front page and BBC's business feed (40 items) read as before.
- Bugs, 2026-11-06 (the owner: "scan for bugs, if you find any, then fix them"). Read from the four cloud newsroom runs and the deploys since the push of 5 November, the owner's open issues, and an independent review of the code changed since Codex's of 1 November (the woman's-name check, the analysis headline, the spokesman and stamp passes); every finding reproduced with a probe, each fix pinned by a self-test that fails on the old code. (1) **«كان … قد» read as a hedge**: the copy desk's guard takes «قد» before a word in «ت» as "may", but a masculine past of the تفاعل and تفعّل forms opens with «ت» too, so «وكان مؤشر ناسداك المركب قد تراجع» (had fallen) refused the desk's plain «وتراجع…» lede in the 06:56 run. `PLUPERFECT` (copydesk.mjs) reads it as the pluperfect when a «كان» opens the same clause within eight words, not after a relative pronoun, with no particle or conjunction between («أن/إن»، «لكنها»، «بينما»…), and never before «تت…», a present; over the archive 45 «قد ت…» now read as "had", each one, and 320 stay hedges. The first version that morning let a relative clause's «كان» switch a real hedge off («الأسعار التي كانت مستقرة قد ترتفع»), caught by the review before it was pushed. (2) **The owner's issues outlived their problem**: the round closed only its "could not fix" issues, so the silent-feed issue still named WIRED a day after WIRED published again; each check it reports to the owner (login, feeds, live site, corrections) now records when it ran clean (`cleared`, editor-precheck.mjs), and `issuesToClose()` (health.mjs) closes those issues. (3) **The spokesman pass of 5 November could lose him**: every earlier pass calls an armed group's spokesman WRONG_SUBJECT, and that refusal excluded his photograph from his own pass, which now forgives the earlier refusals as the neutral pass does (the photographs other stories run and the hard refusals stay out); the civilian filter dropped a record saying "military spokesman" (his pass runs without it); the planner's spokesman is kept out of the story's people in code (as a lead he would run ahead of what his group hit, as on 30 October; as a side figure his own pass would be told he is the wrong person); a rank before his Arabic name («العميد») is read past; and the photo audit, which had no word on him, flagged every one the desk ran (`AUDIT_CLAIMANT`). (4) **`womenBySurname()`** missed «وبحسب يوسف،» (66 «وبحسب» in the archive) and verbs with a shadda («وشدّدت يوسف», 11 of 25 forms of «حذّرت»), and read «ودعت خالد إلى» (she invited Khaled) as a woman named Khaled: marks are read through, «بحسب» takes its «و», and a verb that can take a person as its object counts only after a woman of that family name was introduced; over the archive the same four real hits. (5) Smaller: `stampedSource()` names each agency by its agency form (Isna, a town on the Nile; a photographer called Tasnim); the analysis question check takes «متى»، «أين»، «كم» and «مَنْ» with its sukun. Looked at and left: the push's own deploy, cancelled by GitHub for "a higher priority waiting request" (the next two scheduled deploys carried both commits); a mend refused for bringing back «50», which the revision had cut and the second review never asked for (the rule of 31 October working); a held amateur snapshot of US dollars under the developing countries' debt story (his rule of 30 October); a pluperfect broken by commas («وكان خط شرق-غرب، الذي …، قد تعرض») still read as a hedge (it can only refuse a desk rewrite); the Fed's press feed, quiet since its 28 October FOMC statement but for an enforcement action and an approval it filters out.
