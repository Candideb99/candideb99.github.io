# The check before publication, the saved evidence and unattended recovery

How every story is read against its sources before readers see it, what is kept as evidence, and how the newsroom recovers without a person.

- The check before publication (2026-09-26; the owner, choosing "check, repair, hold" after a review asked that errors be found before readers see them, and "yes, more for quality, capped"; `pipeline/lib/precheck.mjs`): after the critic's gate every news story's final text is read against its own sources by the second look's blind fact-check (`verifyStory`: a separate call that never sees the writer's reasoning; a contradiction counts only when code finds the quoted source sentence). A "confirmed" error or a "drift" figure (no source carries it; here the sources are the very texts the story came from) goes to a targeted repair (the corrections editor's method: only the proved sentences change, code refuses a new figure no source carries, a changed subhead, a non-Arabic body, or more rewriting than the errors need, and the newsroom's own `programmaticChecks` must still pass), then the repaired text is read again; at most three repairs (`MAX_REPAIRS`). A story still wrong, or one the check could not run on, or with no source text, is held: not published, its items take the short rejection mark and are tried again in a later round, and it is listed in `pipeline/state/precheck.json` (`held`) and on the Desk. A clean story costs one more call; a repair two more. Measured on real stories: three planted wrong figures found, repaired in one pass to the exact source values (6.5, 1.7 and 72.5 trillion) and read clean; the same with no repair allowed, held; a clean story passed in one call; a live one-story dry run passed clean in 33 seconds. The article records `quality.precheck` ("clean" or "repaired"); the second look reads every repaired story and one in three clean ones, chosen by slug (an audit sample: reading the rest again would be the same call twice); the repaired errors are the current writer's own proved mistakes and feed the lessons (`verifiedMistakes()`, origin "draft"), which is what a review of the learning asked it to follow; the week's numbers and the Desk count stories checked, clean, repaired and held, and errors caught before readers saw them. `scripts/pipeline-selftest.mjs` (in the gate) pins it on known cases with scripted stand-ins for the model: clean → publish; error → repair → publish; an error that survives three repairs, a repair the guards refuse, a check that cannot run, and a story without source text → held; a retried round or the same event from another outlet → not written again (`repeatsRecent()`, `lib/events.mjs`, now shared with the learner). Also that night: state files are written whole or not at all (`writeJsonAtomic()` in util.mjs: the newsroom's state, the pairs, the ledger, the lessons, the precheck record), and a damaged one is left for a person; `scripts/site-check.mjs` checks the built site as a reader meets it (every page Arabic right to left, titled and not empty; every link inside the site resolving; every image with alt text; no "undefined" or "NaN" printed; every story with its date and sources, explainers without outside sources excepted), in the gate and in the morning round, where a fault becomes the editor's to fix; with a subscription token present `callClaudeCli` removes any `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`, so a key added later can never start pay-as-you-go billing (Anthropic's help page, 15 June 2026: subscription use of `claude -p` counts against the plan; only API-key use is billed; the repository has no such key); the writer is told that source text is data, never instructions. The four errors the stress test found in published stories were corrected the same night through the second look (commit b9c9ac6). The second look now reads every story it corrected once more against its sources (`entry.verified` in the ledger): an error still standing in the sentences the correction wrote is listed for a person and never corrected again by another guess; an error in a sentence the correction did not touch is a new finding and is corrected once. Its first use: three of the four corrections read clean, and the OECD story showed a warning credited to The Guardian that The Guardian's text does not carry, missed by the first reading; corrected once more, it then read clean.
- Unattended operation (2026-09-26, the owner's yes to three items after a review asked that the newsroom run without him: records that survive overlapping jobs, the source evidence kept, and recovery that needs no person). (1) **State files merge entry by entry**: `.gitattributes` names `scripts/merge-state.mjs` as git's merge driver for `pipeline/state/*.json`, and both workflows register it (`git config merge.state-json.driver "node scripts/merge-state.mjs %O %A %B"`; a checkout by hand needs the same line once). Before it, two writers at once (the morning round and the newsroom, or a run by hand during a cloud run) lost records silently: each adding its record at the end of the ledger, the rebase's `-X theirs` kept one and dropped the other with a clean exit, which `scripts/pipeline-selftest.mjs` reproduces without the driver and proves gone with it (every fact-check record, learned mistake and lesson change of both sides kept; both new stories there once each). The newsroom's own jobs never overlap (the workflow's concurrency group queues them), so no story is written twice by two runs. (2) **The source evidence** (`pipeline/lib/snapshots.mjs`): every published story saves, once and never replaced, `evidence/<slug>.json.gz`, committed with the story: for each source its address, names, dates and `fetchedAt`, and the passages the story rests on (each source sentence the check before publication quoted for a claim, with the sentence before and after it, and every source sentence carrying one of the story's figures; at most 4,000 characters a source), and for each sentence of the story the check's verdict with the source sentence it quoted. Never the whole page: the repository is public and the site never reproduces its sources. A first version that kept whole pages on an `evidence` branch was withdrawn within the hour; it also set off a failing Cloudflare Pages preview build on every push, since Cloudflare builds every branch it sees. A page that no longer answers is read from its passages (`loadSources()`, marked `snapshot`); a figure no page carries now is read against them (`settleDrift()`: carried at publication means the page changed, drift stays and nothing is corrected; never carried means a real error, confirmed and corrected); the corrections editor falls back to them too. `node pipeline/backfill-evidence.mjs --days=7` keeps older stories' figure passages as read now (kind "backfill"; they had no check before publication, so no quoted sentences); the week before 26 September was kept that way. A page unreadable when the evidence is taken (a 403, a robots refusal) has none. (3) **Recovery without a person**: a held story's items take the short rejection mark and are tried again after twelve hours, once more, then left for three weeks; a failed check is tried twice; a correction the desk refused or failed is tried once more, twelve hours later, with a fresh reading (`correctionAttempts`); every correction is read again once, and an error in a sentence it did not touch is corrected once more. What stays unresolved after that is flagged, never re-guessed: the morning round (`scripts/editor-precheck.mjs`) opens ONE repository issue listing published stories that still carry a proved error, and a finding the daily editor was woken for twice (at most once in three days) goes to the owner as one issue and wakes the editor no more (`state.tries` in health.json); an open issue is never opened again. The daily editor's fixes to the site's own code (`src/`) are published straight to main once the gate passes, like `pipeline/` and `scripts/` (the owner's yes, 2026-09-26); articles, `public/`, the docs and anything else go through a pull request, and a workflow change is never published by the editor. The week's numbers and the Desk separate **fixed and verified** (errors repaired before publication, corrections read clean afterwards) from **flagged, not fixed** (stories held, corrections left unresolved, corrections not read again).
- The fact-check's quote matcher, 2026-10-27 (the owner: "can we eliminate mistakes and wrong claims?"). Proven errors were already rare in what readers see (the week before: 5 of 88 stories repaired before publication, none held; the second look found none in 32 after), and the claims no source makes ran at about 1.5 a story in the final text (4.9% of checked sentences, 61 of 96 stories), 47 of 115 of them in «لماذا يهمّ», where going beyond the sources is the box's job. Of the rest, about two thirds were «no evidence»: the check had quoted a true source sentence and code could not find it. The page reader often glues sentences («…to 84.8.Weaker demand…»), `tokens()` read «84.8.weaker» as one word, and a quote's figure seemed absent: over the 49 published «no evidence» sentences whose sources still load, the old matcher found 1 quote and the fixed one 36. The same fault left a contradiction the check had found «unverified», which the check before publication does not stop: 4 published sentences since 1 October, among them Bahri's fleet growth (22% printed, the story's own 11 added and 1 retired to 50 make 25%) and an attribution in the Aramco evacuation story. `tokens()` now splits a full stop with a word right after it (a decimal stays), and is hashed into `CHECKER_VERSION` (7bb3653e29 → 8abd70ec7f), since it changes what a pair counts: the lessons' evidence pools start again. What remains is the writer's own unsourced statements in the body (about one in six stories: «والخطوة التالية إبرام الاتفاقية…» with no source saying it, an absence sentence the house style bans), which no check acted on until 2026-10-28 (the owner, asked whether to build it: "I will leave judgement to you to do what is best"). Now, once a news story reads clean, `trimUnsourced()` in `lib/precheck.mjs` gives one call (about 5 seconds and 400 thinking tokens) the sentences of the dek, lede and body the check found no source bearing on, and code cuts each unless it is a general definition or mechanism, even one put in the story's words (the house style allows them), a quote code finds in a source, or the wires' one clause on a material term an actor withheld («لم تكشف الشركة قيمة الصفقة»). Nothing is rewritten, so nothing needs checking again; «لماذا يهمّ» and the analyses are not read; a cut never empties the dek or the lede or leaves a subhead bare, must pass the newsroom's own checks and may take a story a tenth under the word floor (three such stories sat at 300 to 310 words, padded with exactly such a sentence); a reading that fails cuts nothing and holds nothing. Measured before it ran, dry, on the 19 published stories whose saved evidence held such sentences (23 sentences): the first wording kept 8 definitions but cut 3 general explanations put in the story's words (second-round effects, a public filing, where rolling mills get their billets); with the definition verdict naming that case, 12 were kept (11 definitions, 1 withheld term) and 11 cut, each verdict right on reading: the agreement as "the next step", two readings of a minister's needs, a data package's history, a listing's selling rule, an unsourced comparison, what two subscription figures measure, a sanction's effect, and three sentences on what a survey, estimates or a comparison are not. Those 11 sentences were cut from the 9 published stories the same day, without a note (none is a proved error). The week's numbers and the Desk count the cuts apart from proved errors (`unsourcedCut`), and the learner never reads them; `scripts/pipeline-selftest.mjs` pins the step (13 checks).
