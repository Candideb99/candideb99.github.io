# Labels that match their content, corrections and charts

The «الأرقام» box, tags, regions, subject pages, and the tools that correct or redraw a published story.

- Everything sits under a label it matches (the owner, 2026-09-27, on «بغداد ومسقط» and «النجف» under «الأرقام»: "are such mistakes also present in other website sections… posting info that do not match category/title"; two audits of all 189 stories found them everywhere, and a prompt instruction alone had never held a label to its content). Enforced in code where a draft is normalised (`normalizeDraft()` in write.mjs) and over the published stories by `node pipeline/tidy.mjs [--dry-run] [--slugs=]` (code only, no note):
  - the «الأرقام» box (`pipeline/lib/keyfacts.mjs`, also in `serializeArticle()` and `correct.mjs`): a value is a figure that opens with its quantity (digits, numbers in letters, fractions, duals such as «شهران», a measure word as in «نحو 70%»، «حتى 7%», ratings), in Arabic, never a date, a name or a sentence about a figure («تجاوزت 100 دولار…»، «أدنى مستوى في 13 عاماً»); the writers are told so (`FIGURES_RULE`); site-check fails on a published box that breaks it. An explainer's box is its glossary, headed «مفاهيم أساسية» (`KeyFacts.astro` `terms`), and «أرقام اليوم» never draws on it;
  - tags (`pipeline/lib/tags.mjs` `cleanTags()`): one spelling per tag (the site's most used, the definite form, «أمريكي»), and only tags the story's text carries (all of an institution's distinctive words, half of another tag's, or a word it is written with: «النفط» in «برنت»);
  - regions (`groundedRegions()` in `pipeline/lib/regions.mjs`): an Arab desk only when the headline, dek or lede names one of its places (the war named as a cause, «جراء الحرب في الشرق الأوسط», does not count), and a desk the headline names joins;
  - on the site: sub-topics match whole words, headline before tags, the earlier term in the headline winning (`src/lib/topics.ts`; `titleMatch` and `exclude` in `topics.json`); the label above a headline is the story's sub-topic in its short name, or none (`kickerOf()` in topics.ts, since 2026-10-20; until then the tag the headline named first, never a place), and a place never names a subject (`OTHER_PLACES` in regions.ts). Arabic word edges are letters only: «،» «؛» «؟» sit in the Arabic Unicode block and end a word.
  - one page per subject (the owner, 2026-09-27: a subject had lived at up to four addresses, «التضخم والأسعار» 15 stories and «التضخم» 23, «النفط» beside «أسعار النفط», «خام برنت» and «برنت»): a sub-topic's `tags` in `topics.json` (curated by hand; cut to 23 sub-topics on 2026-10-19, below; never a company, an institution, a person, a place or a running event) are its subject, so those tags lead to its page, which lists their stories from every section; a tag that names a section leads to the section; `src/data/tag-aliases.json` gives a running story's second spellings («هرمز» for «مضيق هرمز»). `src/lib/tag-map.mjs` (`tagHref()`) decides where any tag leads and is shared by the kickers, the tag chips, the links in story text (rehype-topic-links.mjs), the front's files row (`threads()`, until 2026-11-09) and the 116 forwarding addresses of the old tag pages (astro.config.mjs `redirects`: tiny pages, not copies of the site); `/tags/` lists only the files and, under المواضيع الثابتة, the subjects. A new subject tag goes into its sub-topic's `tags`; a new spelling of a file into `tag-aliases.json`; `tag-map.mjs` refuses a tag given to two sub-topics.
  - ONE STORY in code too: `style.mjs` refuses a second event stitched in with «وفي تطور منفصل»، «في مجال آخر»، «وفي التاريخ نفسه» and the like (seventeen free-model stories carried one, with their dek, box, «لماذا يهمّ» and tags following it; they were trimmed through `correct.mjs --max-change=0.8`, each with its note). `verify.mjs` drops a chart whose values repeat its own labels. `correct.mjs --why` rewrites a «لماذا يهمّ» about another event without a note; a correction may take away a table or chart of another event. `node pipeline/refile.mjs` moves a story to its right section, with a reason (six free-model stories moved). «لماذا يهمّ» closes the story as its own ruled block (the owner, same day: it read as merged into the article under the lede).
- Corrections, as `/methodology/` promises: `node pipeline/correct.mjs --slug=… --issue="…"` (or `--file=list.json`; `--dry-run` first) re-reads the story's sources, fixes only the reported error wherever it appears, refuses figures that are neither in the sources nor in the report, and adds a dated note to `corrections` (printed at the foot as تصحيح) and `updatedAt`; `--drop-tags=` takes away tags whose subject a correction removed. Eleven stories were corrected this way on 2026-09-23 from an audit's findings. Chart forms (the owner, 2026-10-20: "fix the chart forms in the newsroom too", after the charts were restyled): `CHART_RULE` in write.mjs, exported and shared with rechart.mjs (which had its own copy), is ONE MEASURE PER CHART: the same indicator in the same unit, for one date across countries, companies, sectors or tenors, or over time; never different indicators side by side (its own earlier example had asked for a policy rate beside inflation), a total beside its parts, figures of different dates or windows as if comparable, rules or thresholds as data; actual against expected as two series; a comparison largest first unless its categories have an order of their own. `normalizeChart()` holds what code can see: a grid of «X (فعلي)/(متوقع)» categories is pivoted into series, a total equal to the sum of the others is dropped, categories naming two indicator families (interest, yield, inflation, growth, unemployment; «عائد الإيداع» is a rate) drop the chart, and two categories are allowed only with two series; the self-test pins six cases. Of the 74 charts then published 16 broke it and were redrawn or removed with `rechart.mjs --drop-if-none`. The writers' region lists are read from regions.json (`REGION_NAMES`, `DESK_NAMES`): they had been written into seven prompts and still offered «الشرق الأوسط» a day after that desk was renamed. `node pipeline/rechart.mjs --slug=… --issue="…"` draws a story's chart again from its own text and sources under the chart rule every writer now carries (`CHART_RULE` in write.mjs: the measure readers know, the annual inflation rate and not the monthly change, a percentage as a percentage; the owner, 2026-09-24, on Egypt's inflation charted as -0.4, 0 and 0.1), every figure grounded, no note printed; `verify.mjs` sends back an inflation chart whose values all sit near zero. `--language` fixes a slip of the language (grammar, agreement, spelling) where it stands, with no note and no update stamp, as the desks fix a typo online: at most three sentences may change and no figure may move (the owner, 2026-09-24: "fix the عبرتا grammar slip through the pipeline"; a full desk pass over the 1,400-word في العمق body fixed it but also joined two unnamed officials of different days into one and put the story's comment in a banker's mouth, so it was not kept).
