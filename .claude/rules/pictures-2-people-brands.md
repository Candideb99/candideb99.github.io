Iran's agencies publish under CC BY 4.0 and Commons holds thousands, nearly all stamped (the IRGC spokesman's one usable photograph carries a «MEHR NEWS AGENCY» box), so the IRGC story still gets nothing; the Houthis' spokesman's 2026 podium photograph is clean.
