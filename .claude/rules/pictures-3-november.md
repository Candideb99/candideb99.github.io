- The picture desk, a review of the photographs of 1-6 November (the owner, on «أرباح «العالمية القابضة» الإماراتية تتراجع بنحو الثلث» under a construction crane: "why this article did not use this UAE company logo and just used a crane … if no available photo then scrape from offical company webiste?", then "i do not know what to do. do what is best to fix any photo issues").
