# The picture desk: stand-ins, the city pass, illustrations (6 to 12 November 2026)

Continues pictures-2-people-brands.md. Dated entries; where two differ, the later one stands.

- The picture desk, a review of the photographs of 1-6 November (the owner, on «أرباح «العالمية القابضة» الإماراتية تتراجع بنحو الثلث» under a construction crane: "why this article did not use this UAE company logo and just used a crane … if no available photo then scrape from offical company webiste?", then "i do not know what to do. do what is best to fix any photo issues"). The crane was no bug. The brand pass ran, and the free libraries' one IHC file is a tower crowned «ROYAL GROUP»; Sky News Arabia's photograph of IHC's logo wall was uncredited, so the handout tier passed it over. **A company-website tier was measured and not built.** Over the 20 stories of one company's own news since 30 October (15 companies), with Wikidata's official website (P856) and the newsroom's own reader and robots check: 6 companies had no site on record, 2 matched the wrong body (Qai a Kazakh football club, Emirates the UAE government's portal), 4 sites refuse the reader (OpenAI, Binance, ADNOC; IHC's robots.txt refuses every reader but the search engines), and of the 4 readable only Aramco's offered usable photographs (its facilities; Banque du Caire none, Apple a flat logo, JPMorgan an employee's portrait): about 1 company in 15. Looked at beside their headlines, 43 of the 47 photographs of those days were sound; four were not, and each traced to a rule gap, now closed for every judge, the second check and the audit. (1) **One sector for many** (`ONE_FOR_MANY`): a scene of one business stands in for news that spans several (a holding company's results, a package of agreements) only when the headline or standfirst names it; the IHC crane and solar panels under 23 Oman–China agreements were the last passes' stand-ins. (2) **The story's own vessel** (`ANOTHER_VESSEL`): Nakilat's «موزة» ran under the attack on its «المايده», the check reading the operator as the story's own company; another named ship, aircraft or rig is wrong in every pass but the neutral illustration's, which names none. (3) **People in distress** (`GRAVITY_RULE`): rescuers digging people out of a collapsed building in Idlib ran under a Saudi development loan to Syria; a rescue, the injured, the dead or mourners never illustrate money. Dry runs of the four as new: IHC, Oman–China and the tanker run as text, Syria a treatment room in a Syrian hospital (the standfirst names hospitals; a held snapshot, the last resort). And Unsplash, wired into the desk since 2026-09-24, never ran: the control room's Settings had no box for its key (the server took it). Settings now has «Photo libraries», a key box each for Pexels and Unsplash showing whether each is saved on the laptop and on GitHub; the free Unsplash key is his to make (unsplash.com/developers). Only a paid agency feed would close what remains: company news, named ships, events.
- The picture desk, the same night (the owner, on «جي بي مورغان يدرج الصكوك السعودية في مؤشره لسندات الأسواق الناشئة من 2027», run as text: "why no photo here? bug?"). The judges refused rightly (riyal-sign graphics, note scans, a coin, a studio fan of riyal notes, a Seattle Chase branch whose logo was a few pixels, Jamie Dimon at Davos parties of 2013-2016); the faults were in the search, and one in the name reader. (1) **A change to an index is the index provider's own news**: the planner named JPMorgan as the story's company in one run of three (the newsroom's), the other two reading a market's story, since its list of a company's own news (results, guidance, dividend, deal, listing, penalty, job cuts, product) said nothing of an index, so no brand pass ran. The list now takes a change to an index the company runs, even when that change is news for a market or a country (Al Jazeera ran AFP's photograph of JPMorgan's sign); both dry runs since named it. (2) **The brand pass looks at the head office**: it searched only the planner's «JPMorgan Chase sign» and «JPMorgan Chase bank», Commons holds neither phrase, and the one clear free photograph of the brand, its name in gold over the entrance of its new headquarters at 270 Park Avenue (May 2026, CC BY 4.0), was never seen. `brandPhoto()` now also searches «<company> headquarters», in code; `namesCompany()` reads an entrance, a lobby and the plurals of building and tower as the company's own («the glass-fronted JPMorgan Chase entrance» had read as not naming it). (3) **«CEO of JPMorgan Chase» was a person called Chase**: `RANK`'s «<rank> of X <Name>» form took the company's second word for a name and dropped Dimon's own photograph («Jamie Dimon, CEO of JPMorgan Chase.jpg») from every story about him; one word after «of» and a name is now that name's own («Prime Minister of India Narendra Modi» is still a person). `scripts/images-selftest.mjs` pins all three (169 checks; the three new ones fail on the old code). Dry runs as new after the fixes (n=2): the planner names JPMorgan, the brand pass finds the headquarters photograph and its judge chooses it, and the second check refuses it both times as a front where the name is a small part of the frame (BRAND_RULE: «a street or a building's front where the brand is a small part»), so the story still runs as text. Shown it, the owner ruled «yes run it» (2026-11-07): his name over the company's own entrance, readable at a glance at the printed size, is its own site, as the agencies photograph a bank's or a firm's news ("A sign is displayed outside JPMorgan Chase & Co. headquarters"); `OWN_ENTRANCE` says so in FRONT_RULE, BRAND_RULE, the second check and the audit, and `SMALL_FRONT` keeps refusing a street, a skyline or a front where the name cannot be read at a glance or is one detail among many. He also asked: "i bet there are many free to use photos of jp morgan, why the search results were so limited?" He was right. Commons files 2,175 photographs under JPMorgan's own categories (its branches, towers and the new headquarters, 67 categories three levels deep): 1,221 JPEG, free, 1000 px and landscape, 524 of them taken since 2020; Openverse lists 240 or more each for «JPMorgan Chase» and «Chase bank»; full-text Commons counts were 477 for «JPMorgan Chase», 816 for «JPMorgan Chase bank», 143 for «… headquarters» and none for «… sign». The pass saw about thirty: (a) phrases nobody titles a photograph with; (b) the first two dozen of each, in Commons' text ranking, which puts old towers titled with the name first; (c) only files saying «JPMorgan Chase» kept, so «Chase Bank» branches and «J.P. Morgan» signs were dropped as not naming it (fourteen in the newsroom's run); (d) Openverse, the route to Flickr, answering 504 at that hour (it answered the next day). Now the planner gives the brand's other names on its own signs today (`also`: «J.P. Morgan», «Chase»; `headlineCompany()` keeps up to three, cleaned) and up to two of its Commons categories (`brand_categories`, kept only when one is the company's name or names its brand beside one of its things: «Chase Bank», never «Salmon P. Chase»); `namesBrand()` reads every name wherever the company counts (the brand pass, placedAbroad(), publishedFault(), the reserve), and every judge and the check are told the other names; the brand pass asks for this year's photographs first (`fresh`), browses those categories newest first, holds 24 candidates (was 16), and runs whenever the planner names a company (its head office is searched in code). `scripts/images-selftest.mjs` pins it (173 checks, which its header and this note first gave as 174; each new one fails on the old desk).

- The city pass, 2026-11-07 (the owner, shown Asharq Business's Bloomberg photograph of Downtown Dubai under the same story: "why not then use dubai city photo like bloomberg did?"; then, shown six free Dubai photographs, that a city photograph must show "economic hubs and centers that are distinctive for city and its economic power", recognisable "without reading captions", and his fear of "city photos everywhere"; then "i do not want to manually approve, i want everything to be automated"). The ban was mine: his ruling of 24 September was "a skyline last", and on 28 September I made it "no skyline in any pass, the last resort too" (SKYLINE_LAST); five searches find about 180 free Dubai photographs, and the desk dropped every one. A general city fallback was not built: 33 of the 156 news stories of 24 October to 7 November ran as text, mostly Gulf and Egyptian company and economy stories, and most would have run a skyline. `hubPhoto()` (images.mjs) runs after every specific pass and the reserve, before the last resort, the neutral illustration and any held snapshot, and only for a story about a city's own stock market: `marketCity()` wants an offering, a listing, a first session or the market's index or session in the headline or standfirst, and the exchange named, or the city or the one exchange's country named as the place («في دبي»، «بالقاهرة»); never a bond or a sukuk, and never a city inside a body's name («مجلس أبوظبي للاستثمار»). It searches and browses only the business districts of eight Arab exchange cities (`HUBS`: Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Riyadh, Doha, Kuwait City, Manama, Cairo, Casablanca) and keeps only files whose record names one of their landmarks (`namesHub()`). Its judge and the second check take only a frame a reader would recognise as that city without its caption (its business landmark large and sharp, daylight or a clear dusk, professional; never a distant needle, haze, night, a heritage quarter, a creek, a beach, a marina, a hotel or a mall). It holds no snapshot, and it never runs two in two days (`hubRationed()`, from the stories published with `image.hub`, a new field never printed, and the run's own picks). Over the 461 published news stories the trigger takes 13, every one about a stock market (the Egyptian Exchange's sessions and debuts, the Saudi market, this offering); it refuses Arada entering the Saudi market, Taqa's bonds, Inscale's convertible bonds, Aramco in London, a Dubai tender and Jio's Indian debut. The audit reads a printed city photograph by its `hub`, and its code check no longer calls one a front. `scripts/images-selftest.mjs` pins it (185 checks; the behavioural ones fail on the old desk). Dry runs as new (n=1 each): the Saudi index's jump ran a blue-hour Pexels view of Riyadh with the Kingdom Centre large; Banque du Caire's debut the Iconic Tower and the New Capital's business district in daylight (Commons); DIP's offering at first nothing, since each look of six gave four places to Commons, whose Dubai files are mostly passers-by's, and the judge chose three of them (an HDR phone snapshot, a hazy view from the Burj Khalifa's deck, a tower crowned by Sobha's logo), each refused. In the city pass the shortlist now gives the stock libraries the first three places and Flickr the fourth (`stockFirst`), and the next run took a sharp daytime aerial of Downtown Dubai with the Burj Khalifa large and central (Pexels), captioned «برج خليفة ومنطقة وسط مدينة دبي (أرشيفية)».

- The stand-in for an incident, 2026-11-12 (the owner, on «سفينة تابعة لأدنوك تتضرر في هجوم داخل المياه الإقليمية الإماراتية»: "the picture used here is confusing and not really suitable"). Four gaps, closed for every judge, the check and the search (images.mjs): (1) `KIND_UNSTATED`: a stand-in plainly of one kind (a carrier lettered «LNG», a container ship) is refused under news that happened to a ship, aircraft or plant the story names only by a general word; (2) `NIGHT_RULE` and the check's UNCLEAR: a stand-in is shown in daylight, never a night scene of lights and glare, a frame half black, or a main subject cut by the edge; a record that calls itself a night view ranks behind daylight (`liveliness()`); (3) `standInUnplaced()`: a photograph the check passes as a stand-in (GENERIC_OK) whose record places it in none of the story's countries runs marked «صورة تعبيرية» (it closes a gap in the rule of 23 September: an unplaced stock photograph was never known to be from elsewhere; a file placed at home, the company's own, people and world-desk stories are left alone); (4) the search: the planner asks for an incident in at most one phrase and for the plain thing under way in another («tanker ship at sea»), `neutralQueries()` drops incident words («damaged oil tanker» → «oil tanker»), and the neutral pass takes Pexels' and Unsplash's frames ahead of Flickr's within each search (`collect({ stockFirst })`). Darkness was measured and not made a code check: over 128 published photographs the frame measured 0.64 near-black, between two studio gold-bar frames (0.75, 0.61) that are sound. `scripts/images-selftest.mjs` pins the marking, the ranking, the rules' presence and the searches. Three dry runs of the story as new: the night frame never ran, the new rules were cited in the refusals (an «LPG»-lettered carrier, a chemical tanker, «ODFJELL»), and the story came out as text each time: 7 of the 11 plain tankers at sea Pexels offers already run on other tanker stories, which the repeat guard keeps there. The published story was left as it is (his ruling of 28 October).
