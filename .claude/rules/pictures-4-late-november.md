The desk had searched Morocco about thirty times (Derb Sultan as the place, the writer's and the planner's searches, Commons, Pexels, Openverse; run log `pipeline/runs/2026-11-20T10-38-57-656Z.json`). Four faults of ours lost the country's markets, and one rule of ours let the stranger in. (1) **A rank inside a place's name**: «légumes exposés à Souk Jmiaa à Derb Sultan Casablanca Maroc» (Commons, April 2025, a clear vegetable stall, no people) was dropped from the place tier as showing a person called Casablanca: `RANK` read «Sultan Casablanca» as a ruler. `namedPeople()` now passes over a rank after a place word (`PLACE_BEFORE_RANK`: Derb, Bab, Souk, Rue…) or whose name begins with a place the desk knows (`PHOTO_PLACES`; the first word only, so «Prime Minister Modi Mumbai» keeps Modi), and `NOT_A_PERSON` takes market, souk, bazaar, medina and quarter («Queen Victoria Market» had been a person called Victoria). (2) **The country reader missed the libraries' adjectives and the Maghreb**: `PHOTO_PLACE_RE` read «Indonesia», never «Indonesian», and Morocco was Casablanca and Rabat alone, so the seller «at an Indonesian market» was placed nowhere and Marrakech's stalls were not home. `DEMONYMS` places a photograph by a country's adjective before a place (market, souk, village, port, desert, vendors…, up to two words between); an adjective alone places nothing («Turkish coffee», «Japanese cars», «Egyptian pounds» travel). `PHOTO_PLACES` gains the main cities and French names of Morocco, Algeria and Tunisia, «Türkiye» and Izmir, Giza, Luxor, Aswan, Makkah, Taif and Bali. (3) **A souk was no market**: `relevance()` dropped «Colorful Moroccan souk stalls in Marrakech bustling with local vendors» (Pexels) for naming too little of «Moroccan market», and «Colorful fruit stand in Marrakech offering a variety of fresh produce» of «vegetable market Morocco». `KIN` reads souk, souq, suq, bazaar and stall as market, and produce as vegetable and as fruit. (4) **A photograph of the story's own country that had rested waited behind the stock one**: «"A Moroccan traditional market called "Souk.jpg», a vendor's beans and carrots at a Moroccan souk, ran on 20 October under Morocco's deficit and food prices (30.7 days before), was set aside "only if nothing else is found", and `reused()` came after the neutral pass, which is my order of 19 November. His "real one first" (15 November) and his 30 days cover it: such a photograph (its record or the planner's words place it at home) now comes after the snapshot held back and before any illustration from elsewhere (`restedHome()` in `findImage()`: in place of the reserve's or the last resort's illustration, and before the neutral pass, which is then not made). Every other rested photograph keeps its place at the end. (5) **The rule, ours**: the fallback judge welcomed "shoppers at a stall" and the neutral pass "anonymous people at work" (2026-09-24), and the second check passed her as "a vendor at an ordinary vegetable market". Now `STRANGER_RULE` (part of `PLACE_RULE`, so every judge and the check read it), `NEUTRAL_RULE` and `NEUTRAL_CHECK`: under a story about one country, a photograph that nothing places there shows its subject, never a stranger's face (a vendor, shopper, farmer or worker facing the camera or seen close, a portrait); hands, or people far off or seen from behind, are fine. The second check is told in code when a frame can only stand in (`onlyStandsIn()`, `STRANGER_CHECK` in `photoRules()`; not the audit, which has no record for a stock library's photograph and would call every one a stand-in, so it reads the rule alone), and `strangerFace()` drops before any judge a candidate whose record names a person as its subject and places it in none of the story's countries (in `atHome()` and the neutral and illustration passes; a person the record names, the story's own people, hands, a crowd and world-desk stories are left to the judges). People in a photograph from the story's own country stay welcome: that is the relation to its people he asked for. `scripts/images-selftest.mjs` pins all five (6 checks, 226 in all; each fails on the old desk, run against HEAD's copy in a scratch tree, where the scripted neutral pass picks the Indonesian seller).
