The desk had searched Morocco about thirty times (Derb Sultan as the place, the writer's and the planner's searches, Commons, Pexels, Openverse; run log `pipeline/runs/2026-11-20T10-38-57-656Z.json`). Four faults of ours lost the country's markets, and one rule of ours let the stranger in. (1) **A rank inside a place's name**: «légumes exposés à Souk Jmiaa à Derb Sultan Casablanca Maroc» (Commons, April 2025, a clear vegetable stall, no people) was dropped from the place tier as showing a person called Casablanca: `RANK` read «Sultan Casablanca» as a ruler. `namedPeople()` now passes over a rank after a place word (`PLACE_BEFORE_RANK`: Derb, Bab, Souk, Rue…) or whose name begins with a place the desk knows (`PHOTO_PLACES`; the first word only, so «Prime Minister Modi Mumbai» keeps Modi), and `NOT_A_PERSON` takes market, souk, bazaar, medina and quarter («Queen Victoria Market» had been a person called Victoria). (2) **The country reader missed the libraries' adjectives and the Maghreb**: `PHOTO_PLACE_RE` read «Indonesia», never «Indonesian», and Morocco was Casablanca and Rabat alone, so the seller «at an Indonesian market» was placed nowhere and Marrakech's stalls were not home. `DEMONYMS` places a photograph by a country's adjective before a place (market, souk, village, port, desert, vendors…, up to two words between); an adjective alone places nothing («Turkish coffee», «Japanese cars», «Egyptian pounds» travel). `PHOTO_PLACES` gains the main cities and French names of Morocco, Algeria and Tunisia, «Türkiye» and Izmir, Giza, Luxor, Aswan, Makkah, Taif and Bali. (3) **A souk was no market**: `relevance()` dropped «Colorful Moroccan souk stalls in Marrakech bustling with local vendors» (Pexels) for naming too little of «Moroccan market», and «Colorful fruit stand in Marrakech offering a variety of fresh produce» of «vegetable market Morocco». `KIN` reads souk, souq, suq, bazaar and stall as market, and produce as vegetable and as fruit. (4) **A photograph of the story's own country that had rested waited behind the stock one**: «"A Moroccan traditional market called "Souk.jpg», a vendor's beans and carrots at a Moroccan souk, ran on 20 October under Morocco's deficit and food prices (30.7 days before), was set aside "only if nothing else is found", and `reused()` came after the neutral pass, which is my order of 19 November. His "real one first" (15 November) and his 30 days cover it: such a photograph (its record or the planner's words place it at home) now comes after the snapshot held back and before any illustration from elsewhere (`restedHome()` in `findImage()`: in place of the reserve's or the last resort's illustration, and before the neutral pass, which is then not made). Every other rested photograph keeps its place at the end. (5) **The rule, ours**: the fallback judge welcomed "shoppers at a stall" and the neutral pass "anonymous people at work" (2026-09-24), and the second check passed her as "a vendor at an ordinary vegetable market". Now `STRANGER_RULE` (part of `PLACE_RULE`, so every judge and the check read it), `NEUTRAL_RULE` and `NEUTRAL_CHECK`: under a story about one country, a photograph that nothing places there shows its subject, never a stranger's face (a vendor, shopper, farmer or worker facing the camera or seen close, a portrait); hands, or people far off or seen from behind, are fine. The second check is told in code when a frame can only stand in (`onlyStandsIn()`, `STRANGER_CHECK` in `photoRules()`; not the audit, which has no record for a stock library's photograph and would call every one a stand-in, so it reads the rule alone), and `strangerFace()` drops before any judge a candidate whose record names a person as its subject and places it in none of the story's countries (in `atHome()` and the neutral and illustration passes; a person the record names, the story's own people, hands, a crowd and world-desk stories are left to the judges). People in a photograph from the story's own country stay welcome: that is the relation to its people he asked for. `scripts/images-selftest.mjs` pins all five (6 checks, 226 in all; each fails on the old desk, run against HEAD's copy in a scratch tree, where the scripted neutral pass picks the Indonesian seller). Counted over the archive first: of 577 published photographs, 98 run as «صورة تعبيرية», and 3 of those have a person as their subject by their record (this one, and men at screens under a Qatari and a Saudi market story), so the rule rarely costs a stand-in. Dry run of the story as new (n=1, 13 Claude calls, the newsroom paused): the place tier found Agadir's Souk El Had and Salé's vegetable market (held as naming the topic), the stranger filter dropped six vendors' faces (Ghana, Khulna, Erbil, Pexels) before any judge, and the final comparison ran «A vegetables seller displays vegetables in Morocco.jpg» (Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0): pumpkins, peppers, tomatoes and carrots on a stall, no face, a photograph and not an illustration, captioned «خضر معروضة للبيع لدى أحد الباعة في المغرب». The gap is timing. «ترامب وشي وبوتين يعتزمون زيادة إنتاج المشتقات النفطية» was published 2026-11-19 11:54 UTC with Trump's arrival at Shenzhen (the best then on Commons); the first photographs of the meeting reached Commons at 12:55 (Kremlin, CC BY 4.0), 15:30 and 20:41 (White House, public domain, its Flickr account 202101414@N05), and Commons held about twenty by the 20th. whitehouse.gov/gallery, en.kremlin.ru and the White House Flickr page answer the newsroom's reader from home (GitHub's runners untested; the IEA's wall of 29 October is the warning). Licences: the US government's own photographs are public domain; kremlin.ru's footer licenses all content CC BY 4.0; China's official sites (gov.cn, Xinhua) reserve all rights. An official site is not a free photograph: ministries and presidencies repost agency pictures, and agencies, not governments, are the ones that chase small sites. Recommended to him: a short registry of heads-of-state photo sources with a checked free licence, which the desk reads by the story's people for a recent event, not an open web search (the model cannot tell who owns a page's photograph, and the newsroom's Claude runs without web tools). The timing, read exactly afterwards: the Kremlin's event page says 11:30 Moscow time (08:30 UTC), three and a half hours before the story, but whether its photographs were up then is not known; the White House gallery was published at 20:27 UTC, eight and a half hours after, so only the Kremlin could have served that story.
- The offices' own photographers, built, 2026-11-21 (his "Yes, build it"). `pipeline/lib/official-photos.mjs` holds the registry (`OFFICIAL_SOURCES`: the White House and the Kremlin, each with its leaders, licence, the date and words of the licence check, and a reader for its events and photographs); `officialPhoto()` (images.mjs) runs right after the planner, before the event, place and people passes, for any focus. An office is read when its leader is among the headline's people (the planner's names, or «ترامب»، «فانس»، «بوتين» in the headline when the planner failed: `officialSourcesFor()`); its events from four days before the story to the day after (the story's `publishedAt`, which backfill-images.mjs now passes; the newsroom's own time otherwise) are offered only when one names another of the headline's people, another of the story's countries than the office's own (title and summary; never its place, since every event of a trip to China is placed in Shenzhen, a call to Tajikistan's president among them), or a capitalised word of the planner's `event` («APEC»; never «Summit», «Forum») (`officialEventFits()`); three events an office at most. A Trump story about something done at home reads one listing page and calls no model. The pool is ranked by how many of the headline's people a frame's caption names, and the judge (`twoLooks`, no `gate`) reads `officialTierNote()`: take a frame only when the story reports that occasion, the headline's people together and plain first; otherwise 0.
