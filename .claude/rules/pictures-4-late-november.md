- A stranger's face, and Morocco's own markets lost before any judge, 2026-11-20 (the owner, on «التضخم في المغرب يتسارع إلى 1.9% في أكتوبر، الأعلى منذ فبراير 2025» under a Pexels seller at an Indonesian market, looking into the lens, marked «صورة تعبيرية»: "the people look Asian, the picture did not build actual relation to Moroccan people and emotional relationship that the user can associate. I would have preferred a photo with no people in this case here. But I don't this the news room editor did really search well for Moroccan photos … If it is a bug, then fix it at the source"). The desk had searched Morocco about thirty times (Derb Sultan as the place, the writer's and the planner's searches, Commons, Pexels, Openverse; run log `pipeline/runs/2026-11-20T10-38-57-656Z.json`). Four faults of ours lost the country's markets, and one rule of ours let the stranger in. (1) **A rank inside a place's name**: «légumes exposés à Souk Jmiaa à Derb Sultan Casablanca Maroc» (Commons, April 2025, a clear vegetable stall, no people) was dropped from the place tier as showing a person called Casablanca: `RANK` read «Sultan Casablanca» as a ruler. `namedPeople()` now passes over a rank after a place word (`PLACE_BEFORE_RANK`: Derb, Bab, Souk, Rue…) or whose name begins with a place the desk knows (`PHOTO_PLACES`; the first word only, so «Prime Minister Modi Mumbai» keeps Modi), and `NOT_A_PERSON` takes market, souk, bazaar, medina and quarter («Queen Victoria Market» had been a person called Victoria). (2) **The country reader missed the libraries' adjectives and the Maghreb**: `PHOTO_PLACE_RE` read «Indonesia», never «Indonesian», and Morocco was Casablanca and Rabat alone, so the seller «at an Indonesian market» was placed nowhere and Marrakech's stalls were not home. `DEMONYMS` places a photograph by a country's adjective before a place (market, souk, village, port, desert, vendors…, up to two words between); an adjective alone places nothing («Turkish coffee», «Japanese cars», «Egyptian pounds» travel). `PHOTO_PLACES` gains the main cities and French names of Morocco, Algeria and Tunisia, «Türkiye» and Izmir, Giza, Luxor, Aswan, Makkah, Taif and Bali. (3) **A souk was no market**: `relevance()` dropped «Colorful Moroccan souk stalls in Marrakech bustling with local vendors» (Pexels) for naming too little of «Moroccan market», and «Colorful fruit stand in Marrakech offering a variety of fresh produce» of «vegetable market Morocco». `KIN` reads souk, souq, suq, bazaar and stall as market, and produce as vegetable and as fruit. (4) **A photograph of the story's own country that had rested waited behind the stock one**: «"A Moroccan traditional market called "Souk.jpg», a vendor's beans and carrots at a Moroccan souk, ran on 20 October under Morocco's deficit and food prices (30.7 days before), was set aside "only if nothing else is found", and `reused()` came after the neutral pass, which is my order of 19 November. His "real one first" (15 November) and his 30 days cover it: such a photograph (its record or the planner's words place it at home) now comes after the snapshot held back and before any illustration from elsewhere (`restedHome()` in `findImage()`: in place of the reserve's or the last resort's illustration, and before the neutral pass, which is then not made). Every other rested photograph keeps its place at the end. (5) **The rule, ours**: the fallback judge welcomed "shoppers at a stall" and the neutral pass "anonymous people at work" (2026-09-24), and the second check passed her as "a vendor at an ordinary vegetable market". Now `STRANGER_RULE` (part of `PLACE_RULE`, so every judge and the check read it), `NEUTRAL_RULE` and `NEUTRAL_CHECK`: under a story about one country, a photograph that nothing places there shows its subject, never a stranger's face (a vendor, shopper, farmer or worker facing the camera or seen close, a portrait); hands, or people far off or seen from behind, are fine. The second check is told in code when a frame can only stand in (`onlyStandsIn()`, `STRANGER_CHECK` in `photoRules()`; not the audit, which has no record for a stock library's photograph and would call every one a stand-in, so it reads the rule alone), and `strangerFace()` drops before any judge a candidate whose record names a person as its subject and places it in none of the story's countries (in `atHome()` and the neutral and illustration passes; a person the record names, the story's own people, hands, a crowd and world-desk stories are left to the judges). People in a photograph from the story's own country stay welcome: that is the relation to its people he asked for. `scripts/images-selftest.mjs` pins all five (6 checks, 226 in all; each fails on the old desk, run against HEAD's copy in a scratch tree, where the scripted neutral pass picks the Indonesian seller). Counted over the archive first: of 577 published photographs, 98 run as «صورة تعبيرية», and 3 of those have a person as their subject by their record (this one, and men at screens under a Qatari and a Saudi market story), so the rule rarely costs a stand-in. Dry run of the story as new (n=1, 13 Claude calls, the newsroom paused): the place tier found Agadir's Souk El Had and Salé's vegetable market (held as naming the topic), the stranger filter dropped six vendors' faces (Ghana, Khulna, Erbil, Pexels) before any judge, and the final comparison ran «A vegetables seller displays vegetables in Morocco.jpg» (Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0): pumpkins, peppers, tomatoes and carrots on a stall, no face, a photograph and not an illustration, captioned «خضر معروضة للبيع لدى أحد الباعة في المغرب». The published story was left as it is (his ruling of 28 October).

- The offices' own photographers, built, 2026-11-21 (his "Yes, build it"). `pipeline/lib/official-photos.mjs` holds the registry (`OFFICIAL_SOURCES`: the White House and the Kremlin, each with its leaders, licence, the date and words of the licence check, and a reader for its events and photographs); `officialPhoto()` (images.mjs) runs right after the planner, before the event, place and people passes, for any focus. An office is read when its leader is among the headline's people (the planner's names, or «ترامب»، «فانس»، «بوتين» in the headline when the planner failed: `officialSourcesFor()`); its events from four days before the story to the day after (the story's `publishedAt`, which backfill-images.mjs now passes; the newsroom's own time otherwise) are offered only when one names another of the headline's people, another of the story's countries than the office's own (title and summary; never its place, since every event of a trip to China is placed in Shenzhen, a call to Tajikistan's president among them), or a capitalised word of the planner's `event` («APEC»; never «Summit», «Forum») (`officialEventFits()`); three events an office at most. A Trump story about something done at home reads one listing page and calls no model. The pool is ranked by how many of the headline's people a frame's caption names, and the judge (`twoLooks`, no `gate`) reads `officialTierNote()`: take a frame only when the story reports that occasion, the headline's people together and plain first; otherwise 0. The chosen frame passes the second check as any people photograph does, then `keepOfficial()` fetches its large copy, keeps it as `public/photos/handouts/official-<office>-<number>.jpg` (the Kremlin serves over plain HTTP, which a secure page does not load; HTTPS to it timed out), credits «<photographer or kremlin.ru> · <licence> · البيت الأبيض/الكرملين» (`libraryName`, read by `attributionLine()`), links the event's page, and marks `basis: "official"`, which `backfill-images.mjs --apply` refuses as it refuses a handout. - Japan, the European Commission, and two offices left out, 2026-11-21 (the owner: "add the other offices too, ukraine, EU, india, japan", and whether the readers are code or Claude browsing, and whether to hardcode sites or let Claude search government sites widely). Readers are code (`official-photos.mjs`): reading an office's pages costs no model call; only the judge and the check, as in any photo pass. A registry, not an open search, because the licence is per office (the note above). Each office's licence was read on its own site and each reader tried live with the newsroom's own reader name (util.mjs USER_AGENT), pages and image servers alike. **Japan** (`KANTEI_SOURCE`): «Prime Minister in Action» on japan.kantei.go.jp, its cabinet number read from the home page's link (never written in code: it changes with the prime minister), the latest 20 entries with a description naming who was met, each page's carousel of 980-pixel frames; the Public Data License 1.0 (source cited with the page's address). **European Commission** (`EC_SOURCE`): its Audiovisual Service is an application reading an open interface its own script names (an AWS address; no key); the latest 40 reportages, about five days, each with its people, places and copyright holder; only items the EU holds with no restricted scope, and photographs marked CC BY; 800 pixels to judge, 1,200 kept; «© European Union, 2026 · CC BY 4.0 · المفوضية الأوروبية», as its conditions of use (its item M-002093) ask. **India** (`PMINDIA_SOURCE`): its gallery and PIB's licence («reproduced free of charge… the source… prominently acknowledged»); pmindia.gov.in answers 403 to a reader that names itself a bot or gives an address, so it was held for an hour, until the owner had the reader named plainly (sources-and-coverage.md, the same day); read live then: 7 events of 16-21 November, the SCO forum matched to an SCO story by the planner's event word, 16 photographs, a 51 KB copy for the judge from PIB's server. **Ukraine** (president.gov.ua: «Access Denied» to every request, robots.txt included, an Akamai wall) and **the European Council** (a browser check) are out. Three rules came with them: (1) an office is also read when the headline carries its holder's title (`titles`: «رئيسة الوزراء اليابانية»، «المفوضية الأوروبية»…), not only the planner's name; (2) the office's own people are its listed holders and anyone named in 40% or more of its listed events (`officeOwn()`), so a new prime minister is not taken for a guest and every one of her events matched; (3) an event matches only a country the story names one by one (`countriesByName()`): «الاتحاد الأوروبي» stands for every European country in the place rule, and matched a Commission president's visit to Britain under a story about Poland. Large originals are shown to the judge at 800 pixels (`inlineImage({ maxWidth })`; Japan's frames are 780 KB). `scripts/images-selftest.mjs` pins the three readers, the held India reader, the keys, the triggers and a Japanese summit story through the cascade (10 checks, 250 in all; on the live code the first fails and the block stops, the old code having no Japan reader). Read live from home: Japan's 7 events of 16-21 November, 3 fitting a Takaichi–Xi story, 25 frames; the Commission's 40 reportages, 11 fitting a Poland energy story, 25 frames. No dry run: no published story of the last four days names these offices' holders. Telegram, asked the same day: t.me/s/<channel> pages are public and t.me has no robots file, so a reader is possible at no model cost, but a channel is another door to the same office, its licence unchanged, its frames compressed and mixed with reposted agency pictures; worth it only for an office whose licence is checked and whose own site walls the reader (Ukraine's presidency, whose licence could not be read first-hand).
