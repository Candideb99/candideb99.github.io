# The site, continued

Continues site.md, which reached its size limit on 2026-11-20. Dated entries; where two differ, the later one stands.

- Number, text and redundancy sweep, 2026-11-20 (the owner, after the worked-sum and «…» fixes: "check other pages for similar number and text bugs. also https://khazendar.pages.dev/explainers/ could be buggy since i am not sure it has the best design or organization. also do we need these types tags ... if redundant then remove it also check other website section for similar redundant stuff"). (1) **The sweep**: a probe over all 1,908 built pages (the session's textprobe.mjs) for a summary cut with «…», a sum or Arabic words inside a left-to-right isolation, a figure that reverses printed unisolated in Arabic text, «undefined»/«NaN»/«null» printed, unbalanced «», empty headings and links. One real fault: **a chart's table and its pointer note printed a value with its Arabic unit inside one `<bdi dir="ltr">`** (`Chart.astro`; 230 cells), so «29.9 مليار درهم» read from the right as «مليار درهم 29.9», and the note took the value unescaped. Both now take `isolateNumbers(label(v))`, built into `data-tips` at build time; the note's script no longer wraps it. Not faults: clock times («12:45 بتوقيت القاهرة») and «737-8», «0-9», whose digits hold together across a colon or a hyphen with no space (bidi W4), and «٪», a sign. `readingFaults()` (scripts/site-furniture.mjs) now also fails the gate on Arabic letters inside a left-to-right isolation, and pipeline-selftest.mjs holds the chart's source to the isolated value (the note is built in the reader's browser, where no site check reads it). (2) **«مدخل إلى الاقتصاد» was a news section's page**: one stream of its three kinds newest first, six pages by date, each piece's kind in small type at its foot, and the row «الأنواع» with counts the only place telling them apart (the week's economist was one piece among 61). Now `src/pages/explainers/index.astro`: the newest piece of any kind leads, then a block per kind (`EXPLAINER_KINDS`, src/lib/sections.ts, one list read by the hub, the kind pages and the section bar's panel) under its own name with its newest pieces (6, 3, 3) and its «كل …» link, three ruled columns whatever the count (a lone piece across 1,240px had read as one line); the block heads are the kinds, so the row went; `[section]/[...page].astro` no longer builds the section, so /explainers/2/ to /6/ are gone (the kinds' pages keep every piece). (3) **Counts**: the news sections' «المواضيع», the analysis hub's «المجالات» and the search page's subjects printed each one's count, the archive's bookkeeping he cleared from the front's region block on 18 November; the names stay (on a phone they are the only way to a sub-topic or a desk: the phone menu lists sections only), each pair separated by a light dot of its own (`.page-head__sep`), since without the counts' lighter type the names ran together. The kind pages' «أيضاً:» line had printed «الفكر الاقتصادي ، قراءات»: a «، » between two links in a flex row is an item of its own, with the gap before it; it takes the same dot. Kept on purpose, as before: the counts on /tags/ and a file's intro sentence. Looked at in the built site at 1366px and 375px (no sideways scroll), the chart table read right to left. Live as c3e93d3: the hub and the chart table checked on khazendar.pages.dev; a page the build no longer makes (/explainers/2/) went on answering from Cloudflare's edge, «CF-Cache-Status: HIT» with `s-maxage=604800`, which our `_headers` does not set: Pages keeps a removed page for up to a week, harmless once nothing links to it.
