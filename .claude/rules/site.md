# The site: type, front page, menus, sections

The Astro site in `src/`. DESIGN.md records the exact structure; these are the rulings and measurements behind it. Dated entries; where two differ, the later one stands.

- Keep `lang="ar" dir="rtl"`, logical CSS properties, Western digits, pan-Arab month names. Body font Noto Naskh (justified), headlines and wordmark Amiri, Tajawal only for dates and small furniture. Every font size is a role token in `src/styles/global.css` (`--t-*` heads, `--n-*` reading, `--fs-*` furniture), never a literal: the owner, 2026-09-27, sensed "a circus of fonts", and the site had drifted to 44 sizes while 12.5% of its characters were drawn by system fonts (Tajawal's Latin half and its 800 weight were never ready at first paint). Tajawal has two weights, 500 and 700, all four files preloaded (`Base.astro`) and cached for a year (`public/_headers`); check which fonts really draw the text with the renderer's own report (`CSS.getPlatformFontsForNode`) before and after any type change. DESIGN.md, Typography, has the scale. One house colour, banknote green `--green` (ink, plus the section bar and the logo plate); no section colours, no colour-washed photos, no generated cover art.
- Every article must keep its sources, models and quality fields; the site displays the sources and the automation note, not the model names (the owner, 2026-09-23: «remove such things from the news "النماذج: …"»). A news story credits its outlets in one line, each named once («المصادر: مصراوي، اليوم السابع»), as the desks do («المصدر: الجزيرة + وكالات»), and the line opens the list of reports with their links (`Sources.astro` `credit`; the owner, 2026-11-09, asked whether the sources could be hidden: not required by law, but the list is often a story's only credit, since the house style keeps outlet names out of its sentences; his 2026-10-25 ruling on publishers that block Anthropic rested on the credit; and the repository is public, so a hidden list would still be readable there. He chose the one line). Hub pieces and paper readings keep their open list: there it is the reading, not a credit.
- Sections: economy, markets, energy, companies, technology (news; defence was a sixth until 2026-10-19, folded at the owner's choice: the defence industry is a sub-topic of companies, defence budgets and war costs are economy, the defence feeds file there, `/defense/` forwards to the sub-topic; a news round that had checked out the repository before the fold still filed one story under `defense`, the schema refused it and two deploys stopped until it was refiled: when a section is removed, look for such a story after the rounds then running have pushed), plus the hubs analysis (تحليلات, kinds `analysis` and `weekly`) and explainers (مدخل إلى الاقتصاد, kinds `explainer` and `paper`, the latter a reading of an open-access research paper; an explainer's headline is the question it answers, «لماذا…؟»، «كيف…؟», checked in style.mjs since the owner asked for it on 2026-09-24; an explainer carries a chart of official data when one shows what it explains: `pipeline/lib/datacharts.mjs`, where Claude chooses from a fixed menu of IMF World Economic Outlook and World Bank indicators and Arab countries and code fetches the figures, no key, never from a model's memory; `node pipeline/datachart.mjs --explainers|--slugs=…` adds one to published pieces); `src/data/sections.json` is the list, `kindLabel()` in `src/lib/sections.ts` names a story's kind or section in meta lines.
- The logo is the owner's artwork `brand/logo-source.png` (green calligraphic wordmark over a gold key); `scripts/brand.mjs` traces it into `src/lib/brand.ts` and `public/` (logo.svg, logo-dark.svg, favicon.svg, avatar.png, og-default.png). Run `npm run brand` after replacing the artwork; never redraw the logo and never hand-edit the outputs.
- The masthead (`Masthead.astro`): the logo between two ears, the Gregorian and Hijri dates at the start and the standing tools (الأحدث `/latest/`, لوحة الأسواق, الأجندة, بحث) at the end; the logo is 118px on the front and 64px on every other page (`compact`, from Base.astro), and on a phone every other page opens on the green bar with its small logo (2026-09-27: the header took 35-41% of a laptop's first screen; a story's headline rose from 379px to 266px on a laptop and from 304px to 171px on a phone); the green section bar is a sibling of the header and stays pinned to the top of the screen, taking a small logo and the search once the header has scrolled away (on a phone the search and the menu sit in the bar at all times); on wide screens every section in the green bar opens a content-sized panel: the section's standing sub-topics (a curated taxonomy in `src/data/topics.json`, matched to stories by `src/lib/topics.ts`; each has a page at `/topics/{section}/{id}/`), the section's analyses by desk (`/analysis/desk/{desk}/`), its data pages; the hubs list their kinds (`/explainers/kind/concepts|papers/`). Never fill a menu with the writers' tags: a menu is a taxonomy, edited by hand, and a sub-topic with nothing yet keeps its page (it offers the section's latest instead). The footer has three link columns: الأقسام, الأدوات والبيانات, عن الموقع; never mix tools with the imprint. Every page but the front, /latest/ and the 404 runs «آخر الأخبار» under the market strip (the front from 2026-09-28, when the owner saw its own «أيضاً على خازندار» beside it, asked "is this a bug?" and chose one strip everywhere, until 2026-10-19, when he dropped it there as a duplicate of «الأخبار في سطور»): `Ticker.astro` with `lead="time"`, the eight newest news stories without the page's own, built in `Base.astro` (2026-09-24, as الشرق الأوسط runs its latest-news line). An article's meta line carries both calendars (`formatHijri()`) and its trail (section › the sub-topic it falls under, `subTopicOf()`) with BreadcrumbList data, and its text links the first mention of up to four of its tags that have other stories (`src/lib/rehype-topic-links.mjs`).

on a phone the first screen is one column in reading order and the four stories behind the lead are listed under it (the stage does not turn there); no event takes two places on the front (`repeatedEvents()` in `src/lib/articles.ts`) and no story is printed twice among its story modules (the owner's front-page clean-up, 2026-09-27: each module takes only what no module above it has placed; 21 repeats to none), a feature-plus-briefs block for each section with two or more such stories of the last 72 hours, ordered by its newest story, the thinner sections as one line each under من الأقسام, the الأخبار بحسب المنطقة block as a way in (each desk its count, last update and at most one story not printed above; The label above a headline and the files (`dossiers()`, /tags/) never name a country or a desk (`PLACE_TAGS`): «السعودية» and «الولايات المتحدة» above headlines read as sections of their own, hub blocks. - The site's own functions, the same night (the owner: "check for more bugs and fix them, also websites functionality bugs etc"). The live site was driven in the browser pane (cover tabs and their stop, search with a hostile query, «أ»/«ا» folding and an empty query, the text-size slider, share links, the phone menu, feeds, preview images, 18 page types' requests), and an independent reviewer read every script and endpoint and drove a fresh build in headless Edge. Fixed: (1) **the section bar hid its own start** from about 720 to 790px (iPad portrait among them): `justify-content: center` in a row that scrolls pushes its start past any scroll position, 15-37px of «الرئيسية» or «الاقتصاد» unreachable; two empty auto-margin items now centre it while it fits (Masthead.astro). (2) A phone turned from landscape to portrait kept the cover turning and listed its story twice: the cover now listens to the phone width. (3) A tap on the latest-news strip that missed a link stopped it until the next tap elsewhere (an emulated mouseenter), and a long press (touchcancel) froze it: only a mouse hovers now, and touchcancel releases it; its loop is measured again after a resize. (4) The section panels could not be dismissed without moving the pointer: Escape now hides the open one. (5) «نسخ الرابط» did nothing without a clipboard and said nothing when refused. (6) A page left open past midnight kept yesterday's date line. (7) The search's live region was `display: none` while empty, so its first count could go unannounced. (8) The feed put the lede into its HTML unescaped. Checked with the reviewer's harness on the build (in that session's scratchpad): every section reachable at 768-1366px, the cover still after a turn to portrait, the strip moving on after a tap and after a cancelled touch. The pane's own size emulation fires neither resize nor media-query events, so width changes were tested in headless Edge. Left for launch, design choices: in private mode robots.txt refuses every page, so a crawler never reads the pages' noindex (an outside link can still list a bare address; only access protection blocks that), and the news sitemap is an empty `urlset` when nothing is under 48 hours old.
- The UI sweep of the same request: an independent reviewer drove the live site in headless Edge (19 page types at 360 to 1920px, every internal link, the console, contrast, dates, menus, cover, slider, share, search) and found seven faults, each fixed at its source. (1) The site's isolation of figures (`isolateNumbers()`, bidi.ts) was missing from the search results, the «الأرقام» box's labels, a chart's legend, categories and table, and the titles of Arabic sources, so «البالغ 5-7%» drew «%7-5», «2026-2031» «2031-2026», «(15-30 م³)» «30-15» and «12.7%» «%12.7» beside the same figures printed right elsewhere. The search page is a plain script: it now takes the site's pattern at build time (`define:vars`) with a copy of the function, which the self-test holds equal to bidi.ts's; `site-check.mjs` now fails on a figure printed outside its isolation in those places. (2) «كل مواد مدخل إلى الاقتصاد» under every explainer started 12-49px past a phone's left edge (`.section-head .more` was nowrap; it wraps now). (3) On a phone the section bar left a keyboard-focused section 8 of 72px in view, and the latest-news strip stopped with a focused headline half past its edge: both scroll it into view on focus (the strip's handler sits before the script's early return for a reader who asked for reduced motion, where the first version never ran). (4) «شارك» stood alone above the phone's two share buttons (a 12rem basis in a row sized to its content); the row no longer wraps. (5) A paper reading's source, the paper itself, was headed «مواد ذات صلة في خازندار» and noted as a «شرح»; it takes the story's heading and note. (6) The agenda's «اليوم» and «غداً» were written at build time, so between midnight and the next build (GitHub runs the two-hourly deploy about every six hours) yesterday's decision read «اليوم»; Base.astro's clock now turns them with the date line and drops a past event.
