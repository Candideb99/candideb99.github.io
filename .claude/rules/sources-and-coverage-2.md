# Sources, continued

Continues sources-and-coverage.md (which reached its size limit). Dated entries; where two differ, the later one stands.

- Canada, Brazil, Argentina and Mexico, 2026-11-21 (the owner: "how about argentina and brazil official offices (also news sources, i think i did not see news about them on my website yet). also important countries like canada and mexico etc."). Measured first: since 25 October 2 of 334 stories named Brazil, 2 Canada, none Argentina or Mexico. Two causes: Canada had no source at all, and the Latin American feeds (Bloomberg Línea, El Financiero, Buenos Aires Herald, The Rio Times) had joined two days before. Probed with the newsroom's own readers (`fetchFeed()`, `extractArticle()`, its reader name): a helper agent's survey of about 35 candidates, each one added re-read here before it went in. Added (each noted in `sources.json`): **Canada** CBC (public broadcaster; robots names anthropic-ai and claude-web, read under the 25 October ruling as non-EU), Global News, BNN Bloomberg (`origin` Bloomberg), the Bank of Canada's press releases (official; its general /feed/ lists 2027 placeholders) and Statistics Canada's «The Daily» (official; licence: "worldwide, royalty-free … use, reproduce, publish"); **Brazil** Agência Brasil (a state agency whose terms were read, «Reprodução autorizada para veículos de comunicação com fins jornalísticos, mediante indicação da fonte»; labelled `mustSay` الحكومية/الرسمية; lottery notices left out by title, never «Quina», which «máquina» contains), CNN Brasil (news sitemap, /economia/), InfoMoney (its all-sections feed filtered; its economy feed answers empty); **Argentina** Infobae, Ámbito, Clarín; **Mexico** Expansión, Milenio (news sitemap, /negocios/). Code: Statistics Canada's Atom titles are XHTML (`<title type="xhtml"><div>Retail trade, <span>September 2026</span></div>`), which `textOf()` read as empty, so every entry was dropped as untitled; `deepText()` (feeds.mjs) reads such an element's text in order, its own words before its children's (pinned in pipeline-selftest.mjs). `CANDIDATE_CAP` 995 (run.mjs): the pool held 1,292 fresh items that morning, 73 from the new feeds, given back. Not added: The Globe and Mail, the Financial Post, Estadão, La Nación (subscribers-only; three of them opt out of Anthropic), Valor (subscribers-only and opts out of every Anthropic agent, Claude-User too), Folha (links through a robots-disallowed redirector), CTV (navigation only), Exame (one economy item in three days), El Economista (short bodies behind a paywall script), iProfesional and El Cronista (Argentina has three without them); IBGE and Poder360 (403 to both reader names), the BCRA (its feed answers 500), INDEC and Banxico (no feed), INEGI (empty). Argentina and Mexico still have no official body the reader can follow. No newsroom run has used them yet; the coverage check of 23 November measures the 9 and 19 November sources, and these join its next reading.
