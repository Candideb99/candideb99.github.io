# Sources, feeds, the reports desk and what the editor takes

Which outlets the newsroom reads and why, how pages and reports are read, and the editor's scope. Dated entries; where two differ, the later one stands.

News evidence (2026-10-17): `pipeline/lib/news-evidence.mjs` preserves official-source metadata and
may supplement the selected reports with one relevant institutional document explicitly linked in
their readable bodies. Exact approved hosts, document paths, robots and redirect checks apply;
failure keeps the original evidence, and the five-source limit remains. Publication metadata is
not an event date. The shared editorial brief requires independent construction, honest reporting
origins and sourced expert identification, never invented reporting or novelty. See the dated audit.
- The reports desk (the owner, 2026-10-18: "should the website follow reports like [the UBS Global Wealth Report]?", then "yes build it, however although arab world is in focus (not only gulf states), but important developed economies are also important like germany, france etc"): `--mode=reports` (daily 08:17 UTC; `--report=<id>` for one) reads new editions of the flagship reports in `pipeline/reports.json` from their publishers' own pages, as primary sources (`kind: "report"`, counted like an official source), and writes them through the ordinary path (notes, writer, desk, critic, mend, precheck, picture desk) via `produceStory({ preset })`. `pipeline/lib/reports.mjs`: an edition is detected from LINKS (text, title or address; a future-dated one ignored) or from a page's own text, or from an address pattern (`template`, the IEA's); the edition's page, its full report (a "Full Report" link, a PDF read with `unpdf` 1.8.1 or the IMF's Markdown version; never an annex, a country note, a methodology note or another language: `bestPdf()`) and its press release (`pressRelease()`, for the headline numbers) become up to three sources: the summary, the passages on the economies (`reportExcerpts()`: the Arab economies weighted three to one over the major ones, each tagged [p.N]) and the release. The brief (`reportStory()`) leads with the finding that matters most to Arab readers, all the Arab economies the report covers and then the major economies. State `pipeline/state/reports.json`: a report's first sight records its edition as the starting point unless it is this month's; a failed edition is tried once more the next day; one already told (same address, or the report's name in a recent title) is skipped; at most two a run. 17 reports live, 13 listed as `disabled` with the reason (bot walls: OPEC, OECD, UNCTAD, the Energy Institute, the OBR, ESCWA, the AfDB, the CBO, ifo; a 403: the Arab Monetary Fund; no stable page: the EBRD; the WTO's outlook arrives through its own feed). ## 2026-11-16: state news agencies are not free to republish (SPA read)

The owner asked whether government sites such as spa.gov.sa can be news and picture sources "without worrying about licenses".
