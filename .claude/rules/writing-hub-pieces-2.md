# Hub pieces, continued: «الفكر الاقتصادي»

Continues writing-hub-pieces.md. Dated entries; where two differ, the later one stands.

- «الفكر الاقتصادي» (built as «أفكار اقتصادية»), one economist's idea a week (the owner, 2026-11-20: "should we add in introduction to economics every week a famous economist and explanation of his famous findings/works and its effects? or this will make the website less serious and more like a wiki? also does the intro to economics handle stuff like macroeconomics and microeconomics, developmental economics, plural economics, institutional economics etc?", then "build it"). Measured first: the 48 published explainers followed the week's markets (about 14 oil and energy, 11 bonds, stocks and gold, 9 offerings and company finance, 11 inflation, rates and growth figures, 2-3 trade, 1-2 public finance) and none taught development, institutional, behavioural, labour or heterodox economics; nothing in the explainer editor (`selectExplainerTopic()`) knows a field exists, since it picks the question a reader of the week's news is left with. My advice, which he took: the idea, not the person (a life story is what makes a wiki), tied to a story of the week (his 23 October verdict on news-less explainers), written from sources the checks can hold (a model's memory of a biography cannot be checked), fair to the debate, explainer length.
  - **Kind `idea`**, filed in `explainers`, listed at `/explainers/kind/thought/`, labelled «الفكر الاقتصادي» (`kindLabel()`), with a line under its dek from the catalogue's record, never the model's: «صاحب/صاحبة/أصحاب الفكرة: … (year) · field» (`[slug].astro`, `f: true` marks a woman). `--mode=idea`, Wednesdays 10:29 UTC (newsroom.yml), a button and a coverage row in the control room. On the front it takes the explainer's place in «قراءات خازندار» for its first two days (`freshIdea`, index.astro and the control room's mirror), as «في العمق» took the paper's; it is in the explainers block, the masthead panel and the section's kinds, and out of «بالأرقام» and «أرقام اليوم» (its box is a glossary).
  - **The name** (the owner, the same day, on «أفكار اقتصادية»: "seems like suggestion for projects/business ideas etc"): he chose «الفكر الاقتصادي» from four (with «نظريات اقتصادية», «نظرية وصاحبها», «أعلام الاقتصاد»): the academic term Arab universities use for the field (تاريخ الفكر الاقتصادي), which covers theories, findings and schools and cannot read as business ideas. The page moved to `/explainers/kind/thought/`; the kind stays `idea` in code and files.
  - **The catalogue** `pipeline/ideas.json`: 51 ideas over ten fields (micro, macro and monetary policy, money and finance, trade, development and growth, institutional, behavioural, labour and distribution, public finance and environment, schools and thought: Smith, Marx, Hayek, Schumpeter, Bastiat), each with its school, its author(s) in Arabic and English, year, work, the idea in one line, the news it can explain (`pegs`) and 1-3 reference addresses. Arab thought on purpose: Ibn Khaldun on taxes (his own chapter «في الجباية وسبب قلتها وكثرتها» read from Arabic Wikisource by its `anchor`), al-Maqrizi on debased money, Mahdavy and Beblawi's rentier state; heterodox schools (Minsky, Polanyi, Prebisch–Singer, Veblen, Fama against Shiller). Every address was read with the newsroom's own reader on the day (scratchpad `verify.mjs`): the Nobel committee's popular and press pages, Econlib, the Stanford Encyclopedia, Wikipedia (English and Arabic) and Wikisource read; the IMF answered 429, Britannica 403, CEPR and ECLAC (Prebisch's own papers) 403 and name Anthropic in robots.txt, so none is used; four dead addresses were replaced. `IDEA_HOSTS` (lib/ideas.mjs) names each host on the page; the self-test fails a source on a host not there.
  - **The desk** (`pipeline/lib/ideas.mjs`, `selectIdea()` in select.mjs, `planIdea()`/`writeIdea()` in write.mjs, `runIdea()` in run.mjs): `ideaCandidates()` lists the unrun ideas of the least-covered fields, a whole field at a time until ten (`fieldCounts()` reads the published pieces' `idea.field`); the editor picks one a story of the last seven days shows at work, with a question for the headline naming the economist (its form examples are Gresham and Engel, outside the catalogue, since an example is copied); `readIdeaSources()` reads the references (`excerpt()` cuts a long text around its anchor to 12,000 characters; under 3,000 in all the idea is passed over, up to three times); a researcher's plan (the idea as its author stated it, a picture, two or three steps of which one is what it changed and who disputes it, the numbers, what stays out, the life story first) and the writer, as the explainer's method of 8 November. From there it is an explainer in form (`lessonKind()`): the same desk, checks (the off-plan figure check now reads the plan's `idea` as it reads `insight`), revision, worked example, figure and glossary; its own critic rubric (`CRITIC_RUBRIC.idea`: «سيرة لا فكرة:», «مديح:», «بلا جدل:», a translated quotation in «» a real error, a sentence making the economist speak of today's news a real error); and, unlike an explainer, the check before publication runs, since its facts about the economist must hold. Its sources list on the page is «المصادر», the references then the week's stories.
  - Pinned: `scripts/pipeline-selftest.mjs` (catalogue sanity, the field map, the candidates' order, the excerpt, the reader, the glossary box, the record in the file, the editor's validation, the plan's `idea`); each fails on the old code, which has no ideas.mjs, no `selectIdea` and read only `insight`. The gate passed in the worktree.
  - One dry run (n=1, text only, 14 Claude calls, about 12 minutes, the plan's five-hour window at 37% before it): the editor took Akerlof's market for lemons (micro, every field at 0) and pegged it to «هيئة السوق السعودية تلزم الشركات المدرجة بمناقشة نتائجها علناً مرتين سنوياً» of 19 November; the plan left out his schooling and posts, his other works, the 1970 model's formal details, Gresham, and the Grossman–Stiglitz paradox; the piece (about 750 words): a used phone as the picture, asymmetric information and adverse selection taught with their English once, the Nobel committee's own IT-shares example, a hypothetical worked example (two firms worth 100 and 60 riyals, 80 paid), a five-step chain figure, Spence's signalling and Stiglitz's screening, the CMA rule as regulation (and why it is no signal: it costs strong and weak firms alike), the three journals that refused the paper, and the field evidence that is mixed (Hoffer and Pratt on Wisconsin's used cars). Critic 8, then 9; mended; the check before publication clean. The first critic caught journal names left in Latin, which the revision put in Arabic. Rendered as a page in the worktree and sent to the owner as a picture; nothing published. Rendering it found a site fault: the Latin isolation (`LATIN_SRC`, bidi.ts and rehype-isolate-numbers.mjs) split a run at a colon or comma between two Latin words, so «(The Market for Lemons: Quality Uncertainty…)» drew its halves swapped; a «:», «,» or «;» between two Latin words now keeps the run whole (two published stories carried such a split, «Keefe, Bruyette & Woods» and «Wachtell, Lipton, Rosen & Katz»), pinned in the self-test, which fails on the old pattern. A local build must have `node_modules/.astro/data-store.json` deleted to show a rehype change (the note of 24 October): the first rebuild still showed the split.
- The section carries no photograph, 2026-11-20 (the owner; pictures-3-november.md has the entry and `photoFree()`). Asked the same day whether the writer can draw graphs like supply and demand: it can draw three figures (Diagram.astro, lib/lesson.mjs): supply and demand with one curve shifting (both equilibria, prices and quantities read off the axes as س1/س2، ك1/ك2), flows in and out with their difference, and a chain of two to five causes; plus official-data charts (datacharts.mjs) and the worked example. A local sample of the first was rendered and sent to him. What it cannot draw: a price floor or ceiling (the minimum wage of the Card–Krueger piece fell back to a chain), a tax wedge, a schedule of real prices and quantities, or a textbook curve of another shape (Phillips, Laffer, the production frontier, Lorenz).
- The textbook graphs, 2026-11-20 (the owner, told the writer could not draw a price floor, a tax wedge or the named curves: "check why ... not possible, or you have not really try to make it happen? or it is overly complicated for the reader?", then "Yes, build them"). Not tried: on 8 November three shapes were built (supply and demand with one shift, flows, a chain) and nothing after, and of the 10 explainer and idea pieces since, 9 drew the chain and none supply and demand, the oil-surplus explainer («كيف تتجه سوق النفط إلى فائض…؟») included: offered three, the writer took the one that always fits and has no number to check. Built: `normalizeDiagram()` (lib/lesson.mjs) takes supply and demand with ONE mechanism (`shift` and `shift2` for the other curve; `control` floor or ceiling with its gap; `wedge` tax or subsidy with the trades it costs; two mechanisms refused), a `schedule` (3-8 rows of price, demand, supply; demand never rising and supply never falling with the price; the equilibrium computed by code, `scheduleEquilibrium()`; held to the stories unless `hypothetical`, whole numbers only), and a `curve` of nine named shapes (`CURVE_SHAPES`: laffer, kuznets, phillips with a second curve and a vertical long run, ppf, lorenz, diminishing returns, yield_curve normal/flat/inverted, j_curve, value_function), each with the points it may mark. `src/lib/figures.mjs` holds the geometry (a 640 × 410 drawing, origin at 80, 350) and `Diagram.astro` draws what it returns; the schedule prints its table under the drawing. LESSON_SCHEMA, both planners (step 4 names the graph precisely), both writers and the reviser offer the graphs first and the chain last («only for a sequence of events no graph shows»); the critic's explainer and idea checks write «شكل ضعيف:» for a chain where the field draws a graph, and check the floor above the market price, the ceiling below, the tax on the right side. `partLabels()` (lesson.mjs) is every word a part prints, read by `lessonText()` and, in its own copy, by `lesson-layout.mjs`. `--idea=<id>` runs one idea by hand. All 16 variants were drawn on a temporary page and looked at at 900 px, on a phone (375 px, no overflow) and in dark mode; the first look moved three labels off their curves (the hill's slope labels now sit under the dot, inside the hill; a price control's name at the line's open left end, where a phone's larger type had run it into the supply curve) and gave the schedule round marks and a tighter scale. `scripts/pipeline-selftest.mjs` pins the specs and the drawing's economics (a floor above the market opens a surplus, a ceiling below a shortage, a tax puts the buyer's price above the seller's and cuts the quantity, a subsidy the reverse, a schedule's point where its lines cross, every curve inside its frame); they fail on the old code, which has none of them. No extra model call per piece.
