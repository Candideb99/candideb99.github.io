# Explainers, analyses, paper readings, the weekly and «في العمق»

The hub pieces (kinds explainer, paper, analysis, weekly, feature): how each is written and what the owner said of them. Dated entries; where two differ, the later one stands.

- The week's review (kind `weekly`, label حصاد الأسبوع, section analysis): `node pipeline/run.mjs --mode=weekly`, Fridays 15:37 UTC; written from the paper's own stories of the last seven days plus the calendar of the coming days (which is a check source, so every date is grounded); it leads the column beside the cover while it is the latest analysis.
- Analyses carry a desk (`src/lib/analysis.ts`: the majority section of the stories they cite): تحليل اقتصادي / الأسواق / الطاقة / الشركات / التكنولوجيا, printed as the kicker and meta label wherever an analysis appears, so readings of different kinds are never mixed. The defence desk and its Saturday reading (`--mode=analysis --sections=defense`, 10:07 UTC) went with the defence section on 2026-10-19; in five weeks the reading never found enough defence stories to run. `--sections=` still narrows an analysis by hand.
- «في العمق» is **STOPPED** (the owner, 2026-10-19, of its second piece: "extremely boring to read and i lost concentration, it was poorly written", and its cement-plant picture "weird"; that piece was unpublished). Its fixed form was the fault: a date-by-date retelling of our own stories with a compulsory timeline, a figures section and machine phrases («بدأت تغطية 24 سبتمبر»). The Sunday schedule is removed; the mode remains for a run by hand, which needs his say. What it was: (kind `feature`, section analysis; the owner's Task 5, 2026-09-24, after reading الشرق الأوسط's in-depth pages): `node pipeline/run.mjs --mode=feature`, formerly Sundays 07:47 UTC. The whole story of ONE running file told from Khazendar's own stories: the running files are the tags of the last 45 days' news with five stories or more (not the regional desks, not a file told in the last 21 days); the features editor (`selectFeatureTopic()`) picks one and its question; the writer (`writeFeature()`) tells it oldest first under four subheads (كيف وصلنا إلى هنا، بالأرقام، ما الذي يعنيه للمنطقة، الأسئلة المفتوحة), 1200-1700 words, with a required timeline table (الجدول الزمني: each story's date and what it reported); the checks refuse a missing section or timeline and every figure must trace to the stories. Its title may take the in-depth hinge «الموضوع... ما يظهره الملف». On the front page it takes the research reading's place in قراءات خازندار for three days. Aawsat's investigations and profiles need reporters and are not attempted.

- The explainers' own template (the owner, 2026-10-31, on «ماذا يعني تراجع مؤشر المصانع الصينية إلى 49.3 نقطة؟»: "it feels like this article is badly written ... is this a bug?", then "Yes, fix them"). Not a code fault: three habits our own rules made, measured over all 451 pieces before a rule was written. (1) **A list of questions and answers**: the schema asked for subheads "each the reader's next question", and 32 of the 33 explainers then published headed every section with a question (all 28 rewritten on 24 October among them). Now at most three subheads, each saying what its section shows, at most one a question; `styleIssues()` sends back two question subheads or more (32 explainers, nothing else). (2) **The news told again**: its only material was the news story published 35 minutes before, and "take the reader's next questions in turn" could only be answered from it, so half its body was that story (the Reuters forecast, the holiday spending, the analyst's words, the sectors at 53, the GDP figures). The writer is told NEVER RETELL THE STORY (a story's fact only where it carries the explanation, each once; a question that cannot be answered without the story's causes and outlook is not an explainer's) and to go deeper into the same question; the critic writes «إعادة رواية الخبر:». No code check: word overlap with the stories was high in every explainer, since explaining a figure repeats it. (3) **What things are not**: «لا يأتي… بل…»، «…، لا مقدار…»، «ما الذي لا يقيسه الرقم؟»، «لا.»: seven turns in that piece, a sign of machine writing on the list the owner sent on 23 October. `negativeTurns()` (style.mjs) counts them; four or more in a piece is sent back (7 of 33 explainers and the "robotic" AI-agents reading; no news story or analysis), and an explainer's dek may hold none (5 explainers, each opening «لا،» or a contrast tail); the writer is told SAY WHAT A THING IS, one negative turn at most, and the critic writes «النفي بدل الشرح:». Also: one idea said once (the piece told "more factories fell than rose" four times); a definition stated as the figure is really built (it reduced China's index, a weighted sum of five sub-indices, to one count of answers, which the critic had called optional); and an explainer's glossary, «مفاهيم أساسية», drops a value that is a figure of the news (`normalizeDraft()`: Reuters' forecast there; 5 of 124 published entries). Then, on the dry rewrite those rules produced (the owner: "still find explanations rough and more confusing than explaining anything ... aimed at the layperson and technical people at the same time ... does not connect paragraphs together ... too many contexts in one article, which means we need coherence"): three more causes in our rules. (a) **The paragraphs were forbidden to connect**: the writers' shared house style and the copy desk both said a paragraph opens on its own actor, figure or date, never on a link back (a news rule of 2026-10-19, where a link was the machine stitching its notes); an explanation is a chain of steps, and the rule took out its joints. Both now say it of news only; in an explainer a link that carries the reasoning («ولهذا…»، «وهنا يظهر سبب…») stays, a filler joint goes (the code's `bridgeOpenings()` was already news-only). (b) **Too many ideas**: about eleven in 494 words (the inverted delivery-times index, the day the figure comes out). ONE LINE OF REASONING: the answer planned as three or four steps, each needing the one before, everything else left out however true; 350-550 words (was 450-700). (c) **No picture**: the rules said nothing of abstract noun chains («التحسن أوسع انتشاراً من التراجع»، «مدى اتساع الاتجاه») and forbade an invented example outright (the «وحدات نقدية» of 23 October). TWO READERS AT ONCE: the plain picture first, people and things doing something, then the specialist's term in passing, every sentence with a concrete subject and a verb; and one small round-number picture, said to be one («لو سُئل 100 مصنع…»), when the stories give no figures for a mechanism (a «لو» paragraph is already left out of the number check). The critic writes «خارج الخيط:»، «قفزة:»، «مجرد:». Then, on that dry rewrite's subhead «عتبة 50 نقطة توازن بين الإجابات» (the owner: "looks weird and confusing"): it copied the example our own schema gave («عتبة 50 نقطة نقطة التوازن», itself a string of nouns that said «نقطة» twice), and it named the survey's «الإجابات» before the piece had told of a survey. A subhead is now a short sentence with its verb that the reader understands before he reads its section, in words he already has; the examples are real subheads of other pieces («السوق تحسب موعد إعادة البراميل»، «سعر الشحن صنع قفزة البحري»), so a PMI piece has no PMI wording to copy. The copy desk may reword an explainer's subhead into one (never add or remove one), the critic writes «عنوان فرعي مبهم:», and style.mjs's message and the self-test no longer carry the bad example. No code check: telling a riddle from a sentence needs a reading. A dry rewrite under the rule gave «الإنتاج ينضم إلى الطلبيات تحت العتبة» and «القراءة تحت 50 تكررت في ثلاثة أشهر من أربعة».
