# خازندار — notes for agents

Arabic-first (RTL) economics publication written by an automated newsroom. Read `README.md` first, then `PRODUCT.md` (product truth), `DESIGN.md` (visual system) and `STYLE.md` (how the Arabic is written and how the pages are organised, from the practice of the Arabic economics desks).

- The site is static Astro in `src/`; articles are Markdown in `content/articles/` written only by `pipeline/run.mjs`. Never hand-write news articles; run the pipeline.
- Keep `lang="ar" dir="rtl"`, logical CSS properties, Western digits, pan-Arab month names. Body font Noto Naskh (justified), headlines and wordmark Amiri, Tajawal only for dates and small furniture. One house colour, banknote green `--green` (ink, plus the section bar and the logo plate); no section colours, no colour-washed photos, no generated cover art.
- Editorial rules live in `pipeline/lib/write.mjs` (house style), `pipeline/lib/select.mjs` (news judgement), `pipeline/lib/verify.mjs` (what blocks publication) and `pipeline/lib/copydesk.mjs` (the Arabic copy desk: every draft's headline, dek, lede, «لماذا يهمّ» and body are rewritten out of translationese before the critic, under a guard that counts figures, counts in letters, duals, «» names and Latin tokens over the whole story and matches every hedged claim to its rewrite: a fact may leave a place where it repeats, never the story, and a hedge never becomes an assertion). Since the 2026-09-23 audit of the paper, `pipeline/lib/style.mjs` also checks structure, not only words: a source named in sentence after sentence, a fact retold in dek, lede and first paragraph, stock joints («ويأتي… في وقت»، «، إذ ذكر»), English sentence shapes («ما إذا كان»، «كـ» + noun), idioms translated word for word, the box's formulas, machine leftovers. Change behaviour there, not by editing generated articles; when the owner points at a bad sentence, fix the rule and re-run `node pipeline/copydesk.mjs` over the published articles. The owner's standing rule: no literal translations, ever.
- Every model call is Claude's (the owner, 2026-09-24: «abandon free models and use claude only»; until then only free OpenRouter models were allowed, with Claude first in front of them). `pipeline/lib/llm.mjs` runs every role (editor, writer, desk, critic, vision) on the Claude CLI with the owner's subscription token, `CLAUDE_CODE_OAUTH_TOKEN` from `.env` or the GitHub Actions secret (never commit it); model `KHAZENDAR_CLAUDE_MODEL`, `claude-opus-5-5` (Claude Opus 5.5) by default and pinned by name in `CLAUDE_MODEL`, the workflows and the repository variable (the owner, 2026-09-24: "use claude opus 5.5"; the alias "opus" had been answering as Opus 4.6 under the local Claude Code 2.1.81). Opus 5.5 needs Claude Code 2.1.280 or newer, which ships a native `bin/claude.exe` and no `cli.js`: `callClaudeCli` starts that executable directly (still no shell), and the run report's `llm.byModel` names the model that answered, not the name asked for. There is no fallback model: a call is tried three times and then fails. The picture desk's thumbnails go to the CLI as stream-json image blocks. Never reintroduce a free or other model.
- The Claude CLI (`callClaudeCli` in `pipeline/lib/llm.mjs`) runs the CLI's native executable (`bin/claude.exe` in the npm package since 2.1.2xx; the old `cli.js` under node on older installs) with **no shell**, from a temporary directory, with `--setting-sources ""`. Never launch it through a shell: on Windows cmd.exe cuts a multi-line system prompt to its first word and expands `%…%` inside it (found 2026-09-22: every local Claude call had run with the house style missing). And never call it from the repository directory: print mode pastes this file, the owner's global CLAUDE.md and the agent memories into the writer's context.
- Every article must keep its sources, models and quality fields; the site displays the sources and the automation note, not the model names (the owner, 2026-09-23: «remove such things from the news "النماذج: …"»).
- A story's photograph must come from the story's own country whenever its Commons file says where it was taken (the owner, 2026-09-23, on a Finnish filling station illustrating a US diesel story). `pipeline/lib/images.mjs` enforces it twice: `PLACE_RULE` in every model prompt that chooses or checks a picture, and `placedAbroad()` in code, which reads the file's title, description and categories against the countries the story's headline and tags name (their adjectives and waters too, «مضيق هرمز», and the seats of the world bodies and companies in it; its desks count only when it names none, because a desk is a broad shelf: the Asia desk let a China–Russia story keep an Australian jetty) and refuses a photo placed elsewhere. A story on the world desk alone is left to the models and may keep an honest place in its caption. When nothing from the story's country passes, a neutral frame runs as «صورة تعبيرية» and its caption names no place (the illustration's own caption writer writes that caption before the second check reads it, and the pass has two tries: the first في العمق piece lost a fitting tanker to a caption naming its Norwegian port); `asIllustrationIfElsewhere()` applies that to every pick and `node pipeline/recaption-images.mjs` to published ones. `node pipeline/audit-images.mjs` re-checks every published photo; `node pipeline/backfill-images.mjs --redo=slug,…` re-picks. A story about named people is illustrated with those people first (the owner, 2026-09-23, on the White House facade under a Trump–Xi summit story: "boring"): the writer's first photo search names them, the fallback asks who they are (`people`) and searches them together, a file naming two of them ranks first, an earlier occasion is accepted by both checks (never a wrong person), the geography rule does not apply to them, and `markFilePhoto()` makes an older photo of officials say so in its caption (the occasion's year, or «(أرشيفية)»). Newest first (the owner, same day: "check if we can still get up to date images"): `searchCommons()` keeps the search's own order (`index`; page-id order had put 2017 files ahead of May 2026's), `photoYear()` reads when a photo was taken (record, else file name; never the upload year for the pre-2005 refusal), `recencyScore()` falls off year by year, and the people search asks for this year first. Fresher and livelier (Task 3, 2026-09-24: of the newest 24 stories, 12 ran photos taken 2007-2018, most of them building fronts, skylines, a satellite view or a portrait): the writer's own subjects and the people search also ask for this year's files (`collect()` `fresh`; not generic sector scenes, where "oil pipeline 2026" fetched unrelated files), a story naming two people also searches each of them alone, a mild `liveliness()` weight moves people at work and sectors in action ahead of facades, skylines, satellite views and studio portraits, the judges are told to prefer life (`LIVELY_RULE`), an institution's story may be illustrated by its head at work, captioned as a file photo (`genericQueries()` people), and a person's photo more than eight years old is dropped (`peoplePhoto()`). Relevance leads (2026-09-24, the owner on an offshore platform's risers under a Brent story, "weird", and on the Treasury's front under an explainer of bond yields): the ranking had read recency and life but not relevance, so a 2026 beach beat a fitting tanker; now a file must name at least half of what its search phrase is about (`relevance()`, lower-case words only: capitalised ones are places and names), an event's photograph (forum, conference, ceremony) never serves as a generic scene, every judge refuses a frame that does not read at a glance (`GLANCE_RULE`, `glanceFaults()`, shared with the audit), a piece that explains or analyses is pictured by its subject at work and never by a building front (`CONCEPT_RULE`, the hub writers' `HUB_IMAGE_QUERIES`), and a shortlist the judge turns down whole is passed over for the next four (`twoLooks()`). Captions keep the house spellings (`fixNames()`).
- A news story is written in two stages (`write.mjs`): the desk notes first (`deskNotes()`: 8-20 atomic facts of the one event, each tied to its source and checked against it; a fact copied from an Arabic source or carrying figures its source lacks is dropped), then the story from the notes; with notes in hand the writer sees Arabic sources only through the notes, so it cannot copy their sentences. The critic scores from real errors (a changed figure, direction, period, unit, actor, attribution, or a claim no source makes), never from paraphrase or style, and the second pass is told what was sent for revision. The editor's working headline is dropped when it frames a roundup («أسبوع المصارف المركزية…», «تترقب», «بين … و»).
- Measured against الشرق الأوسط's economy desk on 2026-09-24 (the owner: "can we reach its sophistication level and high quality editorial output even if my website is automated?"): the page design is near parity; the gaps are the news file (their ~30 economy stories a day against our ~7 across all sections, and far more Gulf and Egypt news) and three writing habits now checked in `style.mjs` (an Arab outlet cited for a public fact, a disagreement between sources printed, a large count in raw digits in the headline). A revised draft whose remaining faults are the desk's trade takes one more targeted desk pass instead of being thrown away (`lastDeskPass()` in `run.mjs`); STYLE.md has the detail. `--dry-run --preview=DIR` writes what a run would publish to DIR. The news file, same day with the owner's go (more Claude use accepted): ten Gulf, Egyptian and Moroccan feeds (`regionalNotes` in `sources.json` lists the ones that failed: SPA, WAM, Argaam, Asharq Business…), a daily cap of 20 (was 10), each run takes at least one Gulf or Egypt/Maghreb story until the day holds eight (`ARAB_DAILY`), every feed takes its turn in the editor's list of 300, and price tables are left out (`SERVICE_ITEM`); `feeds.mjs` reads Arabic feed dates and strips Al Riyadh's stray «>» in links. STYLE.md §2 has the detail.
- Mechanics that keep quality without touching articles (2026-09-13 sweep): the editor cites at most five items per story (`select.mjs` cuts more); translated speech is reported, never quoted in «» (`write.mjs` HOUSE_STYLE); the verbatim-overlap issue names the copied passages so the reviser rewrites them; a first-pass "publish" needs score ≥ 6 and clean checks or it takes the revision round; rejected items get a 12-hour mark, then one more chance, then three weeks; a news story needs a fetched body; a run stops taking stories after 35 minutes; explainers state no current events; Commons licences with NC or ND are refused; the copy-desk guard reads through an attached و/ف/ب/ل/ك and accepts the desk's own number-word grammar and thousands separators; the newsroom commits its state after every run, published or not, and a rebase that cannot apply fails the step instead of passing silently; the calendar computes UTC times per date from the institutions' local time zones.
- The logo is the owner's artwork `brand/logo-source.png` (green calligraphic wordmark over a gold key); `scripts/brand.mjs` traces it into `src/lib/brand.ts` and `public/` (logo.svg, logo-dark.svg, favicon.svg, avatar.png, og-default.png). Run `npm run brand` after replacing the artwork; never redraw the logo and never hand-edit the outputs.
- The economic calendar: `pipeline/calendar.mjs` writes `src/data/calendar.json` from the institutions' own schedule pages (Fed FOMC dates, ECB monetary policy meetings, BoE MPC dates, BLS CPI and jobs releases); never from memory, never hand-edited; a source that fails keeps its stored events. `src/lib/calendar.ts` reads it; `Agenda.astro` is the front's module (third column) and `/calendar/` the page. Refreshed with the quotes before every build.
- The week's review (kind `weekly`, label حصاد الأسبوع, section analysis): `node pipeline/run.mjs --mode=weekly`, Fridays 15:37 UTC; written from the paper's own stories of the last seven days plus the calendar of the coming days (which is a check source, so every date is grounded); it leads the column beside the cover while it is the latest analysis.
- The masthead (`Masthead.astro`): the date line carries the Gregorian and Hijri dates and the standing tools (الأحدث `/latest/`, لوحة الأسواق, الأجندة, بحث); the green section bar is a sibling of the header and stays pinned to the top of the screen, taking a small logo and the search once the header has scrolled away (on a phone the search and the menu sit in the bar at all times); on wide screens every section in the green bar opens a content-sized panel: the section's standing sub-topics (a curated taxonomy in `src/data/topics.json`, matched to stories by `src/lib/topics.ts`; each has a page at `/topics/{section}/{id}/`), the section's analyses by desk (`/analysis/desk/{desk}/`), its data pages; the hubs list their kinds (`/explainers/kind/concepts|papers/`). Never fill a menu with the writers' tags: a menu is a taxonomy, edited by hand, and a sub-topic with nothing yet keeps its page (it offers the section's latest instead). The footer has three link columns: الأقسام, الأدوات والبيانات, عن الموقع; never mix tools with the imprint. Every page but the front (its own «أيضاً على خازندار» strip), /latest/ and the 404 runs «آخر الأخبار» under the market strip: `Ticker.astro` with `lead="time"`, the eight newest news stories without the page's own, built in `Base.astro` (2026-09-24, as الشرق الأوسط runs its latest-news line). An article's meta line carries both calendars (`formatHijri()`) and its trail (section › the sub-topic it falls under, `subTopicOf()`) with BreadcrumbList data, and its text links the first mention of up to four of its tags that have other stories (`src/lib/rehype-topic-links.mjs`).
- «في العمق» (kind `feature`, section analysis; the owner's Task 5, 2026-09-24, after reading الشرق الأوسط's in-depth pages): `node pipeline/run.mjs --mode=feature`, Sundays 07:47 UTC. The whole story of ONE running file told from Khazendar's own stories: the running files are the tags of the last 45 days' news with five stories or more (not the regional desks, not a file told in the last 21 days); the features editor (`selectFeatureTopic()`) picks one and its question; the writer (`writeFeature()`) tells it oldest first under four subheads (كيف وصلنا إلى هنا، بالأرقام، ما الذي يعنيه للمنطقة، الأسئلة المفتوحة), 1200-1700 words, with a required timeline table (الجدول الزمني: each story's date and what it reported); the checks refuse a missing section or timeline and every figure must trace to the stories. Its title may take the in-depth hinge «الموضوع... ما يظهره الملف». On the front page it takes the research reading's place in قراءات خازندار for three days. Aawsat's investigations and profiles need reporters and are not attempted.
- Analyses carry a desk (`src/lib/analysis.ts`: the majority section of the stories they cite): تحليل اقتصادي / الأسواق / الطاقة / الشركات / التكنولوجيا / دفاعي وجيوسياسي, printed as the kicker and meta label wherever an analysis appears, so economic and geopolitical readings are never mixed. `--mode=analysis --sections=defense` (Saturdays 10:07 UTC) writes the defence reading from a week of defence stories.
- The newsroom balances sections: a news section with nothing in three days takes its best candidate one point under the threshold in place of the weakest chosen story of a section already served (`run.mjs`, "section balance").
- Sections: economy, markets, energy, companies, technology, defense (news), plus the hubs analysis (تحليلات, kinds `analysis` and `weekly`) and explainers (مدخل إلى الاقتصاد, kinds `explainer` and `paper`, the latter a reading of an open-access research paper; an explainer's headline is the question it answers, «لماذا…؟»، «كيف…؟», checked in style.mjs since the owner asked for it on 2026-09-24); `src/data/sections.json` is the list, `kindLabel()` in `src/lib/sections.ts` names a story's kind or section in meta lines.
- Market data: `pipeline/markets.mjs` writes `src/data/markets.json` (delayed quotes from Yahoo Finance's public chart endpoint, no key; open.er-api.com as the currency fallback; an instrument that fails keeps its last quote marked stale, and the script never fails a build). The deploy workflow refreshes it before every build and rebuilds every two hours on its own; the newsroom commits it as the fallback. `src/lib/markets.ts` formats it; `MarketStrip.astro` runs under the section bar on every page, `MarketBoard.astro` is the compact quote table (the `box` beside the cover, one `group` per market group on the data page), `QuoteCard.astro` a card with a month's sparkline. The data lives on its own page `/markets/data/` (`src/pages/markets/data.astro`: headline cards, five group tables in ruled columns, the latest market news); the الأسواق section itself opens with news and carries a six-card الأسواق في لمحة band under its lead block, as the business dailies do. Never open a section with tables. Never hand-edit the JSON. The rise/fall colours `--up`/`--down` are the one functional colour pair on the site and appear nowhere else.
- The front page (`src/pages/index.astro`) is a news-organisation layout: market strip, the أيضاً على خازندار strip (the owner's label, reworded 2026-09-23: the site is an online news website, never a paper, so no العدد/الطبعة/صحيفة about itself and no issue number; the date line carries the Gregorian and Hijri dates), the tabbed cover (`Cover.astro`, five stories, embedded script, rotation stops on any interaction and never turns while the pointer or keyboard focus is inside or the tab is hidden; beside it قراءات خازندار, one reading of each kind with its dek), three ruled columns (the digest الأخبار في سطور: one sentence per story; secondaries + picks of the last 72 hours; the market box + chart of the day + figures + agenda); on a phone the first screen is one column in reading order and the four stories behind the lead are listed under it (the stage does not turn there); no event takes two places on the front (`repeatedEvents()` in `src/lib/articles.ts`), the ملفات نتابعها band (`Dossiers.astro`, topic tags with 3+ articles), the بالرسوم البيانية band (three compact charts), one feature-plus-briefs block per section, the الأخبار بحسب المنطقة block (the four desks readers see: الخليج، مصر والمغرب العربي، الشرق الأوسط، العالم; `src/lib/regions.ts` folds the writers' finer regions of `src/data/regions.json`, which the writer must use for `regions` and the photo desk reads for geography, so Europe, the Americas, Asia and Africa show as العالم; the owner, 2026-09-24: no Americas desk on an Arab economics site; `node pipeline/normalize-regions.mjs` maps older tags). The label above a headline and the ملفات نتابعها files never name a country or a desk (`PLACE_TAGS`): «السعودية» and «الولايات المتحدة» above headlines read as sections of their own, hub blocks. Keep it dense and picture-led; DESIGN.md records the exact structure. Every story should carry a photo: `pipeline/lib/images.mjs` falls back to a generic Commons illustration, and `node pipeline/backfill-images.mjs` fills older stories.
- Before handing off: `node scripts/gate.mjs` must pass (every pipeline and tool script parses, `astro check` has no errors, `npm run build` succeeds); it records a pass in `.gate/pass.json`, and a Stop hook (`.claude/settings.json` → `scripts/gate-hook.mjs`, this project's sessions only) holds a turn once when a covered file changed since. Added 2026-09-23 after an implicit-any error in a finished-looking change was caught only by a hand-run check; tested on a real type error, which it failed. Run `npm run newsroom:dry` to test pipeline changes without publishing.
- Deploys: pushing `main` to `Candideb99/candideb99.github.io` builds and publishes via GitHub Pages; the newsroom workflow runs on a cron and commits to `main` itself.
- Scheduled automation: `.github/workflows/editor.yml` (daily Claude editor) and `agent.yml` (issue-triggered) both run on the owner's subscription and are gated on the repository variable `KHAZENDAR_EDITOR=1`; `KHAZENDAR_PROVIDER=claude` additionally routes the newsroom's writing to it. Both pass the workflow's own `github_token` (the Claude GitHub App is not installed; its token exchange failed every round from 2026-09-22), and the editor's `--allowedTools` lists exactly the commands its round names: the action allows no shell command by default, and the first rounds died on refused calls. The editor writes `editor-report.md` (gitignored), which the last step publishes as the run summary; the first round that ran to the end was 2026-09-23.
- Claude tokens are never spent twice or for nothing (the owner, 2026-09-23: "make sure the editor does not run continuously and edit already edited articles… It should be smart editor"). The daily editor round runs `scripts/editor-precheck.mjs` first (free: failed newsroom or deploy runs, `npm run check`/`build`, the same event twice in 48 hours, new stories without a picture); a missing picture is filled by `backfill-images.mjs` in a plain step (a few short Claude calls since the free models went, 2026-09-24); the Claude editor is woken only when the pre-check found something only an editor can fix, for that alone, in at most 40 turns. Every article the copy desk has read carries `deskedAt` (the newsroom stamps its own stories as it writes them), and `node pipeline/copydesk.mjs` skips it from then on (`--redo --slugs=…` to edit one again, by name; `--mark-done` records without a model). `recaption-images.mjs` rewrites only captions that break the caption rule unless `--all`; `--spellings` puts every caption in the house spelling («أمريكي»، «ترامب») in code, without a model.
- Corrections, as `/methodology/` promises: `node pipeline/correct.mjs --slug=… --issue="…"` (or `--file=list.json`; `--dry-run` first) re-reads the story's sources, fixes only the reported error wherever it appears, refuses figures that are neither in the sources nor in the report, and adds a dated note to `corrections` (printed at the foot as تصحيح) and `updatedAt`; `--drop-tags=` takes away tags whose subject a correction removed. Eleven stories were corrected this way on 2026-09-23 from an audit's findings. `node pipeline/rechart.mjs --slug=… --issue="…"` draws a story's chart again from its own text and sources under the chart rule every writer now carries (`CHART_RULE` in write.mjs: the measure readers know, the annual inflation rate and not the monthly change, a percentage as a percentage; the owner, 2026-09-24, on Egypt's inflation charted as -0.4, 0 and 0.1), every figure grounded, no note printed; `verify.mjs` sends back an inflation chart whose values all sit near zero. `--language` fixes a slip of the language (grammar, agreement, spelling) where it stands, with no note and no update stamp, as the desks fix a typo online: at most three sentences may change and no figure may move (the owner, 2026-09-24: "fix the عبرتا grammar slip through the pipeline"; a full desk pass over the 1,400-word في العمق body fixed it but also joined two unnamed officials of different days into one and put the story's comment in a banker's mouth, so it was not kept).
- Pre-launch: `site.json` `private: true` forces noindex on every page, a blanket-refusal `robots.txt` and no sitemap. Set it to `false` to launch.
- Photographs are hotlinked from Wikimedia Commons, which serves only the widths 120/250/330/500/960/1280; `src/lib/wikimedia.ts` builds the srcset and any other width answers HTTP 400.
