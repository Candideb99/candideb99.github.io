# خازندار — notes for agents

Arabic-first (RTL) economics publication written by an automated newsroom. Read `README.md` first, then `PRODUCT.md` (product truth), `DESIGN.md` (visual system) and `STYLE.md` (how the Arabic is written and how the pages are organised, from the practice of the Arabic economics desks).

- The site is static Astro in `src/`; articles are Markdown in `content/articles/` written only by `pipeline/run.mjs`. Never hand-write news articles; run the pipeline.
- Keep `lang="ar" dir="rtl"`, logical CSS properties, Western digits, pan-Arab month names. Body font Noto Naskh (justified), headlines and wordmark Amiri, Tajawal only for dates and small furniture. One house colour, banknote green `--green` (ink, plus the section bar and the logo plate); no section colours, no colour-washed photos, no generated cover art.
- Editorial rules live in `pipeline/lib/write.mjs` (house style), `pipeline/lib/select.mjs` (news judgement), `pipeline/lib/verify.mjs` (what blocks publication) and `pipeline/lib/copydesk.mjs` (the Arabic copy desk: every draft is rewritten out of translationese before the critic, under a guard that keeps every figure, count and Latin token intact). Change behaviour there, not by editing generated articles; when the owner points at a bad sentence, fix the rule and re-run `node pipeline/copydesk.mjs` over the published articles. The owner's standing rule: no literal translations, ever.
- Only free OpenRouter models (`:free`) are allowed; chains in `pipeline/lib/llm.mjs`. The key comes from `OPENROUTER_API_KEY` in the environment or the GitHub Actions secret; never commit it.
- Every article must keep its sources, models and quality fields; the site displays them.
- A news story is written in two stages (`write.mjs`): the desk notes first (`deskNotes()`: 8-20 atomic facts of the one event, each tied to its source and checked against it; a fact copied from an Arabic source or carrying figures its source lacks is dropped), then the story from the notes; with notes in hand the writer sees Arabic sources only through the notes, so it cannot copy their sentences. The critic scores from real errors (a changed figure, direction, period, unit, actor, attribution, or a claim no source makes), never from paraphrase or style, and the second pass is told what was sent for revision. The editor's working headline is dropped when it frames a roundup («أسبوع المصارف المركزية…», «تترقب», «بين … و»).
- Mechanics that keep quality without touching articles (2026-09-13 sweep): the editor cites at most five items per story (`select.mjs` cuts more); translated speech is reported, never quoted in «» (`write.mjs` HOUSE_STYLE); the verbatim-overlap issue names the copied passages so the reviser rewrites them; a first-pass "publish" needs score ≥ 6 and clean checks or it takes the revision round; rejected items get a 12-hour mark, then one more chance, then three weeks; a news story needs a fetched body; a run stops taking stories after 35 minutes; explainers state no current events; Commons licences with NC or ND are refused; the copy-desk guard reads through an attached و/ف/ب/ل/ك and accepts the desk's own number-word grammar and thousands separators; the newsroom commits its state after every run, published or not, and a rebase that cannot apply fails the step instead of passing silently; the calendar computes UTC times per date from the institutions' local time zones.
- The logo is the owner's artwork `brand/logo-source.png` (green calligraphic wordmark over a gold key); `scripts/brand.mjs` traces it into `src/lib/brand.ts` and `public/` (logo.svg, logo-dark.svg, favicon.svg, avatar.png, og-default.png). Run `npm run brand` after replacing the artwork; never redraw the logo and never hand-edit the outputs.
- The economic calendar: `pipeline/calendar.mjs` writes `src/data/calendar.json` from the institutions' own schedule pages (Fed FOMC dates, ECB monetary policy meetings, BoE MPC dates, BLS CPI and jobs releases); never from memory, never hand-edited; a source that fails keeps its stored events. `src/lib/calendar.ts` reads it; `Agenda.astro` is the front's module (third column) and `/calendar/` the page. Refreshed with the quotes before every build.
- The week's review (kind `weekly`, label حصاد الأسبوع, section analysis): `node pipeline/run.mjs --mode=weekly`, Fridays 15:37 UTC; written from the paper's own stories of the last seven days plus the calendar of the coming days (which is a check source, so every date is grounded); it leads the column beside the cover while it is the latest analysis.
- The masthead (`Masthead.astro`): the date line carries the standing tools (الأحدث `/latest/`, لوحة الأسواق, الأجندة, بحث); on wide screens every section in the green bar opens a content-sized panel: the section's standing sub-topics (a curated taxonomy in `src/data/topics.json`, matched to stories by `src/lib/topics.ts`; each has a page at `/topics/{section}/{id}/`), the section's analyses by desk (`/analysis/desk/{desk}/`), its data pages; the hubs list their kinds (`/explainers/kind/concepts|papers/`). Never fill a menu with the writers' tags: a menu is a taxonomy, edited by hand, and a sub-topic with nothing yet keeps its page (it offers the section's latest instead). The footer has three link columns: الأقسام, الأدوات والبيانات, عن الصحيفة; never mix tools with the imprint.
- Analyses carry a desk (`src/lib/analysis.ts`: the majority section of the stories they cite): تحليل اقتصادي / الأسواق / الطاقة / الشركات / التكنولوجيا / دفاعي وجيوسياسي, printed as the kicker and meta label wherever an analysis appears, so economic and geopolitical readings are never mixed. `--mode=analysis --sections=defense` (Saturdays 10:07 UTC) writes the defence reading from a week of defence stories.
- The newsroom balances sections: a news section with nothing in three days takes its best candidate one point under the threshold in place of the weakest chosen story of a section already served (`run.mjs`, "section balance").
- Sections: economy, markets, energy, companies, technology, defense (news), plus the hubs analysis (تحليلات, kinds `analysis` and `weekly`) and explainers (مدخل إلى الاقتصاد, kinds `explainer` and `paper`, the latter a reading of an open-access research paper); `src/data/sections.json` is the list, `kindLabel()` in `src/lib/sections.ts` names a story's kind or section in meta lines.
- Market data: `pipeline/markets.mjs` writes `src/data/markets.json` (delayed quotes from Yahoo Finance's public chart endpoint, no key; open.er-api.com as the currency fallback; an instrument that fails keeps its last quote marked stale, and the script never fails a build). The deploy workflow refreshes it before every build and rebuilds every two hours on its own; the newsroom commits it as the fallback. `src/lib/markets.ts` formats it; `MarketStrip.astro` runs under the section bar on every page, `MarketBoard.astro` is the compact quote table (the `box` beside the cover, one `group` per market group on the data page), `QuoteCard.astro` a card with a month's sparkline. The data lives on its own page `/markets/data/` (`src/pages/markets/data.astro`: headline cards, five group tables in ruled columns, the latest market news); the الأسواق section itself opens with news and carries a six-card الأسواق في لمحة band under its lead block, as the business dailies do. Never open a section with tables. Never hand-edit the JSON. The rise/fall colours `--up`/`--down` are the one functional colour pair on the site and appear nowhere else.
- The front page (`src/pages/index.astro`) is a news-organisation layout: market strip, ticker, the tabbed cover (`Cover.astro`, five stories, embedded script, rotation stops on any interaction; beside it قراءات خازندار, one reading of each kind with its dek, over the market box), three ruled columns (the digest الأخبار في سطور: one sentence per story; secondaries + picks; chart of the day + figures), the ملفات نتابعها band (`Dossiers.astro`, topic tags with 3+ articles), the بالرسوم البيانية band (three compact charts), one feature-plus-briefs block per section, the الأخبار بحسب المنطقة block (four desks from `src/data/regions.json`, the list the writer must use for `regions`; `node pipeline/normalize-regions.mjs` maps older tags), hub blocks. Keep it dense and picture-led; DESIGN.md records the exact structure. Every story should carry a photo: `pipeline/lib/images.mjs` falls back to a generic Commons illustration, and `node pipeline/backfill-images.mjs` fills older stories.
- Before handing off: `npm run check` and `npm run build` must pass; run `npm run newsroom:dry` to test pipeline changes without publishing.
- Deploys: pushing `main` to `Candideb99/candideb99.github.io` builds and publishes via GitHub Pages; the newsroom workflow runs on a cron and commits to `main` itself.
- Scheduled automation: `.github/workflows/editor.yml` (daily Claude editor) and `agent.yml` (issue-triggered) both run on the owner's subscription and are gated on the repository variable `KHAZENDAR_EDITOR=1`; `KHAZENDAR_PROVIDER=claude` additionally routes the newsroom's writing to it.
- Pre-launch: `site.json` `private: true` forces noindex on every page, a blanket-refusal `robots.txt` and no sitemap. Set it to `false` to launch.
- Photographs are hotlinked from Wikimedia Commons, which serves only the widths 120/250/330/500/960/1280; `src/lib/wikimedia.ts` builds the srcset and any other width answers HTTP 400.
