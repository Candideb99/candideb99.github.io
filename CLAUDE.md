# خازندار — notes for agents

News evidence (2026-10-17): `pipeline/lib/news-evidence.mjs` preserves official-source metadata and
may supplement the selected reports with one relevant institutional document explicitly linked in
their readable bodies. Exact approved hosts, document paths, robots and redirect checks apply;
failure keeps the original evidence, and the five-source limit remains. Publication metadata is
not an event date. The shared editorial brief requires independent construction, honest reporting
origins and sourced expert identification, never invented reporting or novelty. See the dated audit.

Arabic-first (RTL) economics publication written by an automated newsroom. Read `README.md` first, then `PRODUCT.md` (product truth), `DESIGN.md` (visual system) and `STYLE.md` (how the Arabic is written and how the pages are organised, from the practice of the Arabic economics desks).

- The loops (2026-09-25, the owner: "check if we can improve the website using agentic loops, do deep search. if it would help then implement"): the research (four passes; report in the session scratchpad, "Agentic loops for an AI newsroom") found that a loop improves a newsroom only when something outside the model's own opinion closes it (a source quote code has found, an API, a person), that self-written "lessons" files and extra critic rounds measurably do not help, and that the critic's score had not told right from wrong here (the stories the 23 Sept audit corrected had the same median score, 9, as the clean ones). Built on that: (1) **the second look**, run by every news round before it writes (`pipeline/recheck.mjs --min-age=2 --limit=6 --backlog=2 --budget=12`; `--mode=recheck` by hand; `pipeline/lib/factcheck.mjs`): each news story, analysis, weekly and في العمق piece of the earlier rounds is read again against its re-fetched sources, the newest first (the backlog two a round, free-model stories first; sources that disagree with each other are a "conflict", listed and never corrected toward one of them), one sentence-level verdict per fact with the source sentence quoted; code accepts a contradiction only when it finds the quote, calls a contradicted figure no source now carries "drift" (a page changed after the story passed the figure check), and lists rather than corrects the site's own reasoning (the «لماذا يهمّ» box drawing a consequence) unless the story puts it in a source's mouth; only confirmed hard errors (figure, period, scope, actor, attribution, hedge, term, internal) go to `correct.mjs` (`--report=` names its report), which re-reads the sources and may find the story stands; `pipeline/state/factcheck.json` remembers every story read, so none is paid for twice. Calibrated on the 23 audited stories that rest on sources (pre-correction versions from git): 7 of the 9 audit errors found, plus about twenty real slips the audit missed. (5) **the lessons, learned every news round** (the owner, the same day: "i would make the reinforced learning/self-improvement each time the LLM gets news ... efficient and fast ... i do not have to approve the outcome"; site-level changes such as new sections stay his, by hand): after the second look, `pipeline/learn.mjs` (`pipeline/lib/lessons.mjs`) turns the mistakes it proved and the corrections editor corrected into at most ten lessons in `pipeline/state/lessons.json`, which `run.mjs` hands to the desk notes, the writer and the reviser (`lessons` parameter) and names in each story's `models.lessons`. It is the one form of self-learning the evidence supports: learned only from verified outcomes, each mistake once (its id is kept in `used`), small deltas proposed by Claude and applied by code (add, reinforce, sharpen, retire; a lesson must cite its mistakes, its example is copied from them, never written by the model), a hard cap of ten lessons and 8,000 characters (examples go first, then the least seen), no retirement for silence (a quiet lesson may be the one that works; only the cap moves one out), one call only when there is something new; the fact-check that measures the writer never reads the lessons. Its size does not grow with the mistakes: the first 46 proved mistakes (all in free-model stories) became 7 lessons, 655 words, in two calls of about 25 seconds. Tested the same day (the owner: "check/test if the loop really self-improves or not"): 16 of Claude's newest stories, none of them a source of the lessons, each written twice from the same re-fetched sources and brief (desk notes + writer), without and with the lessons, and both drafts read by the blind fact-check: 7 proved errors without, 3 with (0.44 and 0.19 a story; 2.1 and 0.9 per 100 checked sentences), better on 3 stories, worse on none, tied on 13; a sign test gives p = 0.25, so the right direction, not yet proof (the harness is in the session scratchpad, abtest.mjs). So `run.mjs` keeps a control group: one story in five, chosen by a hash of its working headline, is written without the lessons and named `models.lessons: "control"`, and the harvest compares proved errors per story for Claude's stories with the lessons against the control group and the stories written before them. (6) **Proving it** (the owner, 2026-09-25, after ChatGPT's review: "do 1 and 2 ... how can we make sure that this self-improving loop really gets better?"; two more research passes, notes in the session scratchpad, "proving the loop", with simulations at this newsroom's error rates). What can and cannot be known: most paired stories tie (0.2-0.45 proved errors a story), so whether the lessons help overall can be proven (about 3 weeks if they halve errors, about 10 if they cut a third, on the published control group; 110-150 paired stories for halving), large harm is caught in weeks, and whether one week's change helps (10-20%) cannot be proven at this budget; a first rule that decided week by week would have reverted working lessons about 23 times a year on noise, and was replaced before it ever ran. As built: **evidence pairs at write time** (`evidencePair()` in run.mjs; `pipeline/lib/paired.mjs`): stories chosen by a hash of the working headline, two a day at most, are also written privately from the same sources and brief with the reference lessons, the published first draft being the live side (4 calls a pair); before the launch the reference is the kept version (no lessons until the first promotion; a pair is not paid for while the live lessons are the kept ones), from the launch (`site.json` `private: false`, when the 1-in-5 published control group stops) live, kept and none (7 calls, two a day); every draft goes to the blind fact-check; records in `pipeline/state/pairs.json` with the checker's version, severity (figure/period/scope/actor 3, hedge/attribution/cause/term/superlative 2, else 1, double in the headline or lede), supported and unsupported sentences, words and key facts. **The weekly decision** (`pipeline/lessons-test.mjs`, mode `lessons-test`, Mondays 06:13 UTC, one call): first the canary, up to three errors of different kinds planted by code in a published story the second look had found clean (a changed figure, a weekday moved to the next, a hedge «قد» removed), which the fact-check must call contradicted while calling nothing else in that story contradicted; ≤ 7 of the last 12 found, or 4 false alarms in the last three canaries, is an alarm that stops promotion and learning, never a rollback. Then `lib/evidence.mjs`: the mixture e-value on the discordant pairs since the kept version, anytime-valid (Ville); revert on harm at 10 over all pairs since the kept version, at 20 in the pairs of the text the writer reads now (`lessonsHash`, recorded with every pair), or on stories that say less (supported sentences down more than 10% in more pairs than up) at 20; promote at 20 only when the text the writer reads now has six pairs of its own or more and no more worse than better among them (a second review of the design, the same day: pooled wins of earlier versions must neither crown the newest one nor hide its harm); otherwise keep live as "unproven", evidence carrying over; results pooled only within one `CHECKER_VERSION` (a hash of the fact-check's instructions, its quote matcher and the model). The rule is code, and the gate runs it on 16 known cases (`scripts/evidence-selftest.mjs`, among them "early versions help, the newest harms" and "canary failing, harm: still revert"). `node pipeline/learn.mjs --revert` puts the kept version back at once, by hand, no statistics needed. A revert frees the reverted lessons' mistakes to be learned again; a lesson is reworded at most once a week and only on two new mistakes; a new lesson waits ("pending") until its kind of mistake is proved in a second story that shares no source with the first, or is one serious error (a wrong figure, period, scope or actor in a headline or lede); the learner is shown the retired lessons with the reason each left; code refuses a lesson that tells the writer to leave facts out, unless what it leaves out is what the sources do not support. The run reports count every token (cached reads included) and the price at API rates, and the harvest prints both per story.
