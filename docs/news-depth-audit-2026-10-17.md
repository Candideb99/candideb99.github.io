# Depth and reader comprehension: Egypt rating report

The owner correctly identified weak journalism despite the previous critic's publish verdict:
an unidentified analyst, vague wording, unexplained rating symbols, thin development and repetitive
paragraph openings. A model score did not establish reader quality.

The cover's third position is intentional. `pickLead()` considers importance, age and a photograph;
the following four slots select the strongest of the newest twelve stories. At this review the
Trump waiver and Brent stories carried importance 9, against Egypt's 8. The newest-first news
strip is separate. No cover ordering or rotation behaviour was changed.

The original Al-Borsa report identifies Mahmoud Nagla as its quoted fixed-income/money-market
executive at Al Ahly Financial Investments. It discusses the conditions under which investors may
accept lower yields, with observations on sovereign-risk pricing and financing channels. The
published synthesis selected too little of that explanation and omitted his role. The desk notes
now prioritise concise sourced identification, definitions, comparisons, mechanisms and limits
within the existing twenty-fact cap. Rich reports and their revisions receive 450-700-word guidance;
this is not a new rejection threshold, a required length or permission to pad.

Sources read for the rating explanation:

- [Moody's: Understanding credit ratings](https://www.moodys.com/web/en/us/solutions/ratings/understanding-ratings.html).
  The official page explains relative credit risk and category meanings. Its default Readability
  extraction returned only the B/Caa/Ca/C fragment, so it was not silently treated as a complete
  rating scale by the runtime enrichment.
- [Fidelity: Bond ratings](https://www.fidelity.com/learning-center/investment-products/fixed-income-bonds/bond-ratings).
  The fetched body includes creditworthiness, the investment/speculative distinction and the scale
  containing B3, Caa1 and Baa3. It is fetched for matching rating stories, marked as reference
  material, and excluded from the event's body/corroboration checks. It cannot turn a single
  unconfirmed news report into a confirmed event. Failure preserves the original reports; total
  source items remain capped at five.
- [Al-Borsa: the financing implications](https://www.alborsaanews.com/2026/10/17/1999905).
  The page was read through the site's extractor when the web reader could not open it.

«الترقية» is understandable as financial usage, but «رفع التصنيف» identifies the action more
precisely. «بمقدار محدد» conveys a qualification about the size of a potential cost reduction, but
is abstract without that relation. The shared brief asks for a clear faithful paraphrase, retaining
the uncertainty rather than claiming that funding automatically becomes cheaper.

Initial waw is legitimate. The existing warning excluded وقال/وأضاف/وأوضح and therefore missed
the owner's concrete four-paragraph example. A new warning includes repetitive reporting openers;
it asks for substantive regrouping and rhythm, not a ban on waw or automatic connector substitution.

The critic's existing observations now explicitly check necessary expert identification and central
term explanations when supplied. Two synthetic calibration cases contrast an accurate but opaque
rating digest with an explained report; these test critic recognition, not overall writing quality.

The live rewrite's first critic requested the missing expert identification despite a score of 10.
The notes prompt alone had still not retained it reliably. An optional identifications list now
reserves up to three sourced expert descriptions beside reader context within the same twenty-fact
budget. They pass the existing source-number, Arabic and overlap checks; an invalid declared
identification requests a concise faithful rewrite rather than silently disappearing. Older answers
without this optional list still parse, and a source without an expert role must not invent one.

The first locally prepared rewrite passed its critic and factual check, but editorial reading still
found the investment-grade cutoff unexplained in risk terms and an unnecessary unexplained CDS
passage. It was not pushed. The brief now explicitly distinguishes explaining a category from
listing its code boundary, and asks the writer to leave out ancillary jargon that the supplied
sources cannot explain. A further calibration case tests that gap. These observed failures are why
automated acceptance alone is not presented as proof of seasoned writing.

The initial supposedly clean synthetic fixture explicitly denied that its event occurred and lost
the origin of an analyst's comments. The critic correctly requested revision; the fixture was
repaired rather than weakening the critic. The explained version then passed, and the version
with an unidentified expert still requested revision. This is curated calibration, not a quality rate.

An explicit single-story refresh reuses the normal newsroom gate, preserves identity and publication
metadata, and prints a dated editorial update. Original publication evidence remains immutable;
refresh evidence receives its own snapshot. No manual rewriting of generated article text, broader
unpublishing, publication threshold change or cover reordering is part of this repair.
