# Autonomous writing and the significance box

The owner's examples exposed failures in finished journalism: «وفق تعريف المرجع» tells the
reader about the desk's research rather than defining the concept; a box narrated by Mahmoud
Nagla restates an analyst's contribution instead of giving Khazendar's reading. The previous
critic's high score did not establish reader clarity or editorial quality. The active cloud
provider was verified as Codex / GPT-6.1 Sol, with high reasoning, rather than Gemini.

## Cause and change

The writer demanded attribution for every claim, while its significance box was expected to
provide meaning. The critic called a consequence absent from a source an error. The factual
checker already allowed the website's bounded reasoning. A learned attribution lesson also
said to add no conclusion nobody drew. These instructions were inconsistent.

`WHY_POLICY` now distinguishes reported premises from the site's limited inference. Writer,
copy desk, critic and why-only repairs share it. Learned rules continue to protect reported
facts and attribution; they do not prohibit the specifically permitted synthesis. Forecasts,
invented premises, borrowed analyst opinions and ungrounded causal links remain faults.
The writer supplies one to four source-indexed premises. Code validates their basic shape,
source indexes and numeric grounding; the critic must independently check their actual meaning.
These checks do not prove that a premise is semantically true.

The style checker flags vague process attribution and analyst-led significance boxes. The
critic evaluates the box's premises, reasoning and limits within its existing reader check.
The publication score, verdict rules and number of revision rounds are unchanged. A brief
remains brief when evidence is limited; no length quota or invented context creates depth.

The owner explicitly removed public «تحديث تحريري» banners for style improvements. A named
refresh records its reason in the run audit, retaining its original date/address and previous
corrections. Factual corrections still carry dated correction notes.

## Evaluation method

The known Egypt article was edited through the copy desk and a why-only repair, not hand-written.
That repair is not evidence about autonomous future writing. Regression tests cover the actual
vague phrase, an analyst-led box, valid reporting attribution, the legitimate benchmark sense of
«مرجع», invalid premise source indexes and unsupported numbers.

A dry run selected three previously unseen feed events without a bespoke angle or human revision
instructions: the US budget deficit, Abu Dhabi's digital-government platform and Indonesia's
index-membership risk. It uses the normal selector, notes, writing, desk, critic/revision and
factual check, skipping pictures only. Inputs, raw/final drafts and reviews stay in system temp,
outside the public repository. The existing private-site lessons/control assignment still applies.

`scripts/writing-eval.mjs` compares anonymous raw/final texts with a separate reader prompt,
without the production critic's scores or diagnosis. It uses the same model, so it is a diagnostic,
not an independent human assessment or proof of elite-publication quality. Its findings never
revise or publish the test inputs. A three-event sample cannot establish long-run reliability.

## Observed result and limits

The run selected all three events, but only the US deficit report completed. Its first critic
requested revision for an attribution error and an untranslated law name. The automatic revision
removed those defects; the second critic returned publish/10 and the factual precheck returned
clean. Its significance box used the site's voice and distinguished gross tariff receipts from
receipts after refunds. This is one diagnostic result, not proof of dependable quality, and a
10/10 score is not a literary assessment. No test story was published.

Abu Dhabi's notes completed, then the writer was refused: “Your workspace is out of credits.”
The Indonesia case hit the same refusal. The run recorded ten successful calls and nine failed
attempts. The separate reader evaluation was therefore not run. No API fallback or credit
purchase was used. A new offline regression verifies that this exact workspace refusal now
stops the run without repeated retries, a guessed quota reset or marking the event rejected.
Workspace credits are not assumed to be the five-hour or weekly subscription allowance.

The dry run used the initial shared synthesis policy. Subsequent wording clarifications scoped
the older learned attribution lesson and the body-closing rule to reported claims. Those final
prompt clarifications have offline/build validation; they have not had a fresh full live writing
test because of the service refusal. Three-event autonomous quality remains unverified.
